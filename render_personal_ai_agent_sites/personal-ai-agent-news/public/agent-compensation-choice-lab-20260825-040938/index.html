Recover honestly

The best failure record is actionable: it says what happened, what was undone, and what remains.

Compensate only by contract

Use a compensating action when its scope and side effects are known, not because “undo” sounds comforting.

Retry only when safe

Repeat work when idempotency and failure modes are understood. Otherwise a retry can multiply the damage.

Persist partial truth

When neither path is safe, record the exact partial state and hand the next decision to an authorized owner.

This simulation is a teaching model, not a production recovery policy. It demonstrates a contract-first pattern: assess reversibility, bound retries, and preserve partial state.
