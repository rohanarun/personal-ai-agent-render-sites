Randomize election timeouts

Followers wait for heartbeats. Different timeout deadlines reduce the chance that several candidates start simultaneously.

deadlineᵢ ∈ [T, T + spread]

Vote once per term

A node grants at most one vote in a term, and only to an eligible candidate. A majority cannot support two different leaders.

quorum = floor(n / 2) + 1

Step down on a newer term

Any message carrying a higher term makes an older leader or candidate become a follower. Terms fence stale authority.

incomingTerm > term ⇒ follower

Agent-system actions

  • Persist current term and vote before replying.
  • Use heartbeats shorter than election timeouts.
  • Require majority connectivity for leadership.
  • Keep side effects behind committed log entries.
