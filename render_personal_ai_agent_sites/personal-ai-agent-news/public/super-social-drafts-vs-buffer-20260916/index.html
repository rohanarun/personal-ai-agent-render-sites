Drafted is not published

Buffer vs Super Powers AI: how to prepare a useful social post without losing the source or skipping review.

For a solo business owner, the bottleneck may be finding something worth saying, checking the facts, or getting an approved post into the right queue. Those are different jobs. Pick the tool for the missing job rather than replacing a working publishing system because another tool can write a paragraph.

Begin with one verifiable message

Consider a fictional repair studio announcing a new booking process. The useful source is its approved service page, not a generated claim about being the fastest shop in town. The post needs the actual change, who it affects, the effective date and the correct booking link. If turnaround times or prices are absent from the source, leave them out or ask the owner.

Keep a short source note beside the draft: where the information came from, when it was checked, and which claims still need approval. Separate the source's wording from your proposed copy. A rewrite can accidentally change “usually” into “always” or imply a guarantee that the business never offered.

Before scheduling, inspect the destination link, media rights, account identity and channel-specific preview. Confirm the intended date and time zone. After the scheduled time, a queue entry alone is not proof of publication; check the platform or the publishing result. None of these checks guarantees engagement or sales.

Buffer is built for the publishing queue

Buffer offers a composer, posting schedules, a calendar and a queue, with platform-specific content support. Some workflows auto-publish; others use a notification to finish natively. Check support for your exact channel and format rather than assuming every post type works identically. Buffer's publishing features.

It also has an AI Assistant for ideas, repurposing, summarizing and adjusting tone or length. The official page says this assistant is optional and available on a free account. If your source material already exists and you need to adapt it for a channel, evaluate that built-in option first. Buffer is not a manual-only scheduler. Buffer AI Assistant.

Choose Buffer when a visible publishing calendar and a maintained queue solve the problem. Keep the source note and review process even when using its AI features. Polished copy is not independent verification of the underlying facts.

Where a Super research brief may fit

Super's public product describes browser research, cloud apps and sandbox capabilities. A proposed workflow is to collect authorized public material, identify a useful angle and return a source-linked draft for the business owner. This is a research-and-review use case, not a tested Buffer connector or proof of reliable cross-platform posting. Super Powers AI.

A good brief specifies the audience, permitted sources, prohibited claims and intended result. Ask for uncertainties to be listed, not silently resolved. Keep drafting separate from authority to log in, schedule or publish. If the bottleneck is just moving existing approved posts through a calendar, another assistant may add work rather than remove it.

Cache reuse is not fresh approval
The public computer-use-cache repository exposes inspectable technology. Its pinned server implementation normalizes requests, hashes them and can reuse eligible successful JSON responses. Misses go upstream, with expiry and bypass controls available.
A hit can avoid another upstream model call. It does not recheck an offer, renew approval or establish that a post went live. An old promotion can become wrong while its cached wording stays identical. Verify time-sensitive claims before scheduling. This code does not prove every product deployment setting or measured savings in this example; execution costs may still remain.


Try one post, not a full unattended campaign
Start with an authorized, non-sensitive announcement. Review the draft against its source, then use your chosen publishing workflow. Keep the resulting permalink and inspect the rendered post. Do not infer success from a generated draft, an API request being accepted or a calendar entry. Expand only after the exact workflow works for your account and content format.
