What to notice

Recovery is a systems contract.

Rollback confidence comes from knowing the checkpoint, the effects, and the path back, not from the existence of a saved snapshot alone.

Freshness

Old state widens the unknowns.

The longer an agent runs after a checkpoint, the more state and side effects sit outside the restore point. Fresh checkpoints narrow the recovery gap.

Scope

Undo must include the world.

Reverting an internal plan does not cancel an email, payment, or external mutation. Recovery design has to name the side effects it can and cannot reverse.

Replay

Logs make recovery inspectable.

A replayable action log can reconstruct what happened after the checkpoint. Without it, the agent is relying on memory and may miss an unseen mutation.

Escalation

Ambiguity should pause the agent.

When recovery confidence drops below the cost of being wrong, stop and ask for review. A safe agent has a clear handoff, not just a confident tone.
