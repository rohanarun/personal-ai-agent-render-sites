n8n: an explicit workflow you operate

n8n documents visual workflow building, custom code, app connections, managed hosting and self-hosting. Its product page also describes execution inspection and human-in-the-loop controls. It is a strong candidate to investigate when your team wants to build and maintain the workflow itself.

That does not prove a particular integration or deployment meets your needs. Check its exact actions, hosting requirements and plan limits. Self-hosting also leaves operational responsibilities to resolve.

Super: delegate a supported task

Super describes an assistant using its own cloud apps, browser and sandboxes. Its computer-use cache is intended to reuse successful app actions. Investigate it when you want a bounded task performed by an assistant, while retaining review of the outcome.

We do not establish self-hosting parity, n8n workflow import, or support for every private system here. Those are questions to verify, not features to assume. Neither approach removes the need for permission and output checks.

What the cache does and does not establish

First-party demonstration, not an n8n comparison test.

Inspected local-execution lookup code requires an owner and task context. It looks for cached workflows and returns verification information with a match, or an explicit miss. The caller handles misses; the lookup itself does not execute fallback work.

Reusable actions are actual implementation, but source inspection does not prove every production task follows that route. No relative speed, cost or reliability result was measured. A reused action still needs an appropriate task, current inputs and an acceptable result.
