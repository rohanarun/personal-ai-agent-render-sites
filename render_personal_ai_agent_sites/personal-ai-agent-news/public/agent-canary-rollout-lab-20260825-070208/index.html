Three habits for staged change.

Make the rollout itself a measurable, reversible experiment.

Define the stop signal

Choose the observable regression that pauses expansion before the change becomes the new normal.

Keep rollback real

A button is not a rollback plan unless the previous behavior, state, and ownership are ready to return.

Expand on evidence

Increase exposure when the change performs across the signals that matter, not only when no alarm fired.
