Keep the lesson decision human
Choose the specific occurrences you want learners to supply. Repeated words are separate choices: omitting the first occurrence does not omit the second. Blank numbering follows passage order, not the order in which you select words. Punctuation and spacing remain from the entered text.
Review the student passage before sharing it. A blank can have more than one reasonable answer; this answer key records only the original selected words, not every acceptable response. This tool does not grade, choose learning objectives, infer reading level or demonstrate an educational outcome.
The browser's Intl.Segmenter identifies word-like segments using the selected language. Segmentation may vary by browser and language data. Numbers may count as word-like; punctuation-only segments are not selectable. Review the offered boundaries, especially for names, compounds and non-space-separated writing.
Use a passage you have permission to adapt. Keep student names and private records out of the example. Text is processed in this page, without upload or model calls. Reloading loses edits. Download the student file for learners and keep the separate answer file with your teaching materials.
