Code-unit order
Agree on the language convention
Illustrative entries include accented words and numbered terms. Swedish and German can place ä differently relative to z. Numeric ordering changes how digit sequences compare; it does not rewrite the entries or turn them into numbers.
Each nonempty line is an entry. Exactly empty lines are omitted; spaces within or around an entry are preserved and participate in comparison. Duplicates remain. Full collation sensitivity is used, and entries that compare equal retain their input order. No translation, deduplication or editorial correction is performed.
A client may require a house style that differs from a browser's locale data. Confirm that convention and inspect the export. The JSON record includes input entries, both orderings and resolved collation options, not just the final list. Browser versions and locale support can affect ordering; this is not a universal dictionary standard.
