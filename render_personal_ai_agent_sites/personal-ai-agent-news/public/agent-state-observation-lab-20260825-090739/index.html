Observe before you commit.

An agent does not act on reality. It acts on an observation of reality, which may be stale, partial, or too coarse for the decision in front of it.

State checks are part of the action plan whenever the world can change underneath the agent.

Refresh the state, then act.

The observation is useful but not reliable enough to carry through a consequential action without a refresh.

A fresh observation is a control, not a courtesy.

Match scope to action

The state needed for a read-only answer differs from the state needed to mutate a shared system. Observe what the action depends on.

Expose staleness

Record when state was observed and what could have changed since. Time is part of the evidence.

Recheck at the edge

Before a consequential action, refresh the precondition that makes the action valid. Do not trust an old snapshot by habit.
