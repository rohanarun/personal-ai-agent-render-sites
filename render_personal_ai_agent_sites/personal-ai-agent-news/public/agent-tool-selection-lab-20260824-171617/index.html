What the model shows

Selection is part of safe execution.

Run a scenario to see how a tool choice can become consequential before the action begins. Inspect when the cost of being wrong is larger than the cost of slowing down.

  • Candidate quality describes how much confidence the agent has in its available options.
  • Action cost makes uncertainty matter: a cheap reversible choice is different from an irreversible one.
  • When risk is high, inspect the options and state the reason for choosing one.
