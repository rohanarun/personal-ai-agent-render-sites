One rate is not the whole story

The dispersed preset flips A1 and B2. The clustered preset flips A1 and A2. Both disagree on 2 of 8 labels, but the latter concentrates all observed disagreement in group A. This tiny constructed example reveals a pattern; it cannot establish a cause or a population-level bias.

What is being counted?

Each example adds one to C[reference][observed]. Disagreement = (C[0][1] + C[1][0]) / 8. Group rates divide each group's mismatches by its four examples. Reference labels are stipulated truth only in this toy dataset. In a real audit, a reference can also be wrong; disagreement alone is not proof of an annotation error.

Matrix convention follows scikit-learn's confusion-matrix definition, with observed annotations substituted for predicted labels. Calculations here are direct counts implemented locally, not a call to scikit-learn.
