A certificate, not just a falling curve

For this fixed discrete linear system, both eigenvalues are a, strictly inside the unit circle. Solving P - A transpose P A = I gives a unique symmetric positive definite P. Then V(x) = x transpose P x decreases by exactly ||x|| squared at each update. This is the discrete Lyapunov result in Bof, Carli and Schenato, Section 3, Theorem 3.2.

V(x(0)) = sum from t=0 to N-1 of ||x(t)|| squared + V(x(N))

The two plots use different units and vertical scales. Coupling can transfer the second coordinate into the first, raising Euclidean magnitude even while the certificate energy falls. P changes when A changes; comparing raw energies across different matrices is not comparing the same metric.

Scope: one stable, time-invariant, two-dimensional linear update. This certificate does not establish stability for arbitrary switching, a nonlinear learning process, or an agent that changes its update rule.
