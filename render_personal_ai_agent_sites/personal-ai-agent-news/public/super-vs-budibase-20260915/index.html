Build an app, or delegate a task?
Budibase
Budibase combines end-user apps, data, automations, and agent-building features. It is a candidate when a team needs to design and maintain its own internal interface and workflows. Its Agents offering is labeled beta in the product navigation. Official product overview.
The documentation distinguishes editing an app from making it available to end users: a published workspace and a live app are involved. Verify the real deployment process rather than treating this page's one-button example as its specification. App lifecycle documentation.
Super Powers AI
Super presents an assistant with its own apps, browsers, and sandboxes for delegated execution. It is a candidate when completing work across existing interfaces matters more than building a new app UI. Test the exact workflow, account access, and final result before relying on it. Official product overview.
Neither an assistant request nor a saved automation proves that a draft was actually published. A pilot should inspect the end-user surface after the action, not only the editor or a success message.
Pricing needs a deployment choice
Budibase lists cloud Pro, Premium, Business, and Enterprise plans, with creator, end-user, action, AI-credit, and log-retention details. Its separate open-source self-hosted offering is listed as free with community support; hosting and operations remain your responsibility. Cloud end-user add-ons are listed at $5 per user per month. Confirm the billing toggle, quotas, and add-ons on the current pricing page before comparing totals.
Super lists pay-as-you-go wallet usage and optional managed-device plans. Those are not equivalent to Budibase creator or end-user seats. Compare the full cost of your actual workload, not just a plan headline. Super pricing.


What can be reused?
Super's first-party cache release describes successful response reuse for exact repeated requests. A miss goes upstream. Reusing a response avoids another model call, but execution and infrastructure costs can remain.
The separately inspected workflow-cache code requires verified completion, task context, an owner, and runnable Python steps before storage; portability checks can decline saving. That is executable workflow reuse, not the same thing as caching a response. Source inspection does not prove availability on every account or measured savings for this form task.
A cached procedure does not make a previously observed publication state current. Recheck the published form and a representative submission. This page does not establish Budibase integration, comparative cache performance, privacy guarantees, or universal app compatibility.
Define the pilot result
Use non-sensitive sample data. Decide who can publish, what validation changes, how old submissions are handled, and what rollback means. In this local model, whitespace-only notes fail only when the published rule requires a note. Labels are display text; changing them does not migrate earlier records. Reloading discards all state. The rehearsal permits twenty publications and twenty accepted records before reset.
