Before replacing a working scenario, decide what you want to stop maintaining. Moving a connected workflow and delegating a single task are not interchangeable projects.

Keep Make for visual orchestration

Make describes visual, code, and prompt-based automation with app integrations and AI agents. If seeing and managing a multi-step workflow is central to your work, staying with Make remains a reasonable option. That is a fit judgment based on its documented product, not a performance claim.

Consider Zapier Agents for connected business work

Zapier Agents documents company-knowledge connections and work across its app ecosystem. It is worth evaluating when your alternative should act on existing business-app data. Confirm the exact action, permissions, limits and failure behavior; a broad app list alone does not prove parity with your scenario.

Consider Super for supported delegated tasks

Super Powers AI describes an assistant with cloud apps, browser access and sandboxes, plus a computer-use cache for reusable app actions. It is an option to investigate when you want a supported task carried out by an assistant. This is not a claim that it imports or replaces arbitrary Make scenarios.
