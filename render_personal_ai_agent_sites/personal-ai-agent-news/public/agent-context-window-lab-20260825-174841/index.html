Context action

Retain with a concise label

retain = min(relevance, recency, signal, sensitivity)

Why the minimum?

A highly relevant detail can still be stale or unsafe to carry forward. Taking the minimum makes context selection sensitive to the weakest reason to keep it and makes summarization a deliberate action instead of silent accumulation.
