Equal error, until a coordinate saturates

For independent zero-mean Gaussian coordinates, the optimal allocation is di = min(level, variancei). Choose the level so the allocated errors sum to D. A saturated coordinate has zero rate: reconstructing its mean already achieves its variance as error.

R(D) = sum 0.5 log2(variance_i / d_i)
D = fraction * sum variance_i

The proportional baseline uses di = fraction * variancei at the same total error. These are asymptotic rate-distortion limits, not a finite-file compressor. At zero distortion the rate diverges; the plot starts at 5%.

Computation: quadprog solves the equivalent capped equal-level allocation by minimizing one half the sum of squared distortions, with fixed sum D and coordinate bounds. This surrogate finds the same allocation; it is not the rate objective. At the zero-rate endpoint the smallest valid water level is displayed.
