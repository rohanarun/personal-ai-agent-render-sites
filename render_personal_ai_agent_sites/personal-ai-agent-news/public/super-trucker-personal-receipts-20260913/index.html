Personal receipt organizer for truckers

Sort small personal purchases during time off. Keep employer expenses, dispatch information and sensitive receipt details out of this local scratchpad. Use only while safely parked and off duty.

Published by Super Powers AI. Reviewed September 13, 2026. Personal organization, not accounting or tax advice.

One currency, one personal ledger

Amounts are USD. Start empty, or load an explicitly illustrative example. This page does not read receipt images or connect to a bank.

Nothing is saved across reloads or sent to Super. Export before leaving. The CSV contains dates, categories and amounts only; it is not an original receipt.

Keep a useful boundary

Illustrative scenario: after a trip, you want a personal overview of meals and laundry purchases. Add each amount once, reconcile against the original receipt, then export. A category is only your organizational label, not a deductible-expense decision. Keep originals separately.

Refunds, multiple currencies, reimbursement, mileage, hours of service and business accounting are outside this tool. Do not use its total as a complete financial record.
