Make recovery explicit

A robust agent can explain what changed between attempts, what limit applies, and why escalation is safer than repeating an uninformative action.

  • Record the failure reason and the next hypothesis.
  • Require new evidence before retrying a side effect.
  • Use a bounded retry budget with a clear handoff.

Try the trap

Push failures and impact high while keeping evidence low. The right behavior is not “try harder”; it is to stop spending authority without a reason.

Do not loop on hopeA retry should earn its place with new information, not elapsed time.
