Remote team time handoff

Agree on the instant, not an ambiguous “nine tomorrow.” A dated time brief for distributed teammates, without converting every city by hand.

The input is UTC, not your device's local time. Results include the local date and UTC offset. No invitation is sent and no availability is checked.

A date belongs with every time

Illustrative scenario: a coordinator in New York sends a check-in to London and Kolkata. One instant can land on different calendar days. Export the dated brief, then ask teammates to confirm their availability before creating the event.

Daylight-saving offsets depend on the date. This brief describes one occurrence, not a recurring schedule. Browser time-zone data supplies the conversion; check critical future arrangements against the actual calendar invite. The tool does not infer a person's location or working hours.
