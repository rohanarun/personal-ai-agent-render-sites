Pack together without the guessing

A shared-trip handoff for friends or families: record who owns each item, which bag it belongs in, and what still needs packing. This is one local checklist, not a synchronized group workspace.

Illustrative starting items, not a complete packing recommendation. Edits stay in this open page; export before closing. No names or checklist entries are sent to Super.

A handoff, not a travel clearance

Assigning an item does not prove it is in the bag. The checkbox records your own confirmation. Before departure, compare the remaining-items export with the physical bags and ask each person to confirm their portion.

This tool does not check baggage allowance, item restrictions, weather, destination requirements, weight, or medical needs. Use the relevant official sources for those decisions. A bag label here is simply a label, not an airline category.

Where Super can enter the workflow

You could separately ask Super to research an airline's official baggage page for a specific itinerary, with source links and a date. That request is not executed here. Review the source yourself before relying on it, and confirm supported tasks and current charges on Super's product page.
