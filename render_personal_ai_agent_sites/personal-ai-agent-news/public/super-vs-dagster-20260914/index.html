Where Dagster fits
Dagster describes itself as a data orchestrator for data engineers, with asset lineage, observability and a declarative programming model. Its Python asset definitions describe dependencies between data assets. This is a documented fit when the central problem is understanding and operating a data pipeline. Dagster overview.
Its asset checks test data properties such as schema, missing values or freshness, with results shown in the UI. Checks do not count toward Dagster+ credit usage according to its current guide. Whether a failed check prevents a later operation depends on how the workflow is configured; a green field in this editor is not that configuration. Asset-check documentation.
Dagster's July 13, 2026 acquisition announcement says Dagster remains supported under its existing name and open-source license, with Dagster+ continuing commercially. It says no customer migration is required as a result of that announcement. This page does not infer completed transaction details beyond the notice. Official Prefect announcement.


Where Super fits
Super Powers AI presents an assistant with its own apps. Its computer-use workflow-cache implementation, inspected for this page, stores eligible verified procedures with runnable steps and can return a matching workflow with evidence. This is procedure reuse, not simply answer caching. Super product page.
Storage requires an owner, task or source context, verified-complete confirmation and runnable steps; portability checks can decline it. Lookup returns a miss for missing context, no entries or no safe match. The caller needs to handle that miss; the endpoint alone does not establish automatic fallback. A hit includes helper-version context, verification reason and output evidence. Matching may incur usage charges.
This supports evaluating Super for repeated application work around an asset handoff. It does not prove feature parity with Dagster's asset catalog, orchestration, or data-quality controls. Nor does it demonstrate current account access, successful execution, or a measured speedup. Test the exact application and review requirements before adoption.


Costs and the decision
Dagster's current pay-as-you-go FAQ lists Solo at $10/month plus $0.040 per credit, and Starter at $100/month plus $0.035 per credit. Serverless compute adds $0.010 per minute; Hybrid has no Dagster serverless compute charge, but your infrastructure costs remain separate. Pro is sales-priced. Credits count asset materializations and executed ops. Check your contract and the live pricing page rather than treating base fees as total cost. Dagster pricing and May 2026 pricing notice.
No equivalent Super total-cost quote is established here. Compare actual workload usage, infrastructure, review effort and failure recovery. For data-asset orchestration, Dagster is the more directly documented fit in these sources. For a repeated application action, evaluate Super against a real pilot. A combined workflow is an option, not an integration claimed to ship out of the box.
