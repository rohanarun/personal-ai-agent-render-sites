Uncertainty and reversibility tell different stories.

A decision can be uncertain but safe to test, or certain but dangerous to undo. Map both dimensions before an agent chooses its next move.

Four quadrants, four habits.

Routing is useful when each quadrant has a concrete behavior attached to it.

Investigate

Take a small information-gathering step when uncertainty is high but feedback is cheap and safe.

Ask

Clarify intent when multiple interpretations could lead to materially different actions or recipients.

Act or abstain

Act when impact is bounded and rollback is real; abstain when a wrong move can outrun both.
