Super vs Dovetail

Turn feedback into a brief without turning repetition into proof.

A support ticket, a sales note and an interview all mention the same issue. That sounds like three customer signals. But what if they all came from one account? Before deciding which tool should summarize the material, decide what the evidence actually supports.

When Dovetail is the direct fit

Dovetail describes a customer-intelligence platform for calls, tickets and research, with AI projects, chat, source-linked insights, documents and agents. It also describes API, MCP and CLI access. Its positioning is not simply storage for transcripts. If your need is ongoing customer-evidence analysis inside a shared environment, evaluate that specialist workflow first. Official product overview.

The homepage labels AI Channels 2.0 and AI Dashboards as beta. Check availability, plan requirements and your own sources before relying on any feature. This article does not reproduce the vendor's ROI claims or treat them as results for your team.

Where Super could add preparation work

Super publicly describes browser access, cloud sandboxes and automated research. A proposed workflow is to combine authorized feedback excerpts with public product documentation, identify unresolved questions and draft a decision brief. That may fit work spanning several kinds of source. It does not establish a tested Dovetail connector, evidence repository or permission-preserving synchronization path. Super product description.

For example, a product manager might ask whether an export complaint reflects missing functionality, a discoverability problem or an outdated help article. Super could be evaluated for preparing the comparison; the manager still needs to inspect the original feedback and current product behavior. A general assistant is not automatically better at this than Dovetail's own AI features.

A brief that can survive review

  1. Define the decision. “Should we investigate export discoverability?” is different from “Should we rebuild export?” State the product version, audience and decision owner before asking for a synthesis.
  2. Preserve the evidence unit. Keep source identifiers, dates and account or participant linkage where authorized. Repeated records may be useful context, but should not silently become independent customers. Retain a link back to the source rather than copying unnecessary personal data.
  3. Ask for alternatives. A proposed Super instruction: “Using these authorized excerpts and public docs, separate observed statements, interpretations and missing evidence. Identify possible duplicate accounts. Do not infer customer prevalence. List checks needed before a recommendation.”
  4. Review the recommendation separately. Verify the source match, the current product behavior and relevant counterexamples. Record who made the decision. Neither a generated document nor a source citation is approval to change the roadmap.

A useful first pilot uses a small, de-identified set you have permission to process. Track unsupported statements and correction effort, not just how quickly a draft appears. If linking or permissions cannot be preserved across tools, keep analysis within the approved environment.

Cache reuse is not new evidence

Super's public Computer-Use Cache is inspectable technology. The reviewed proxy revision hashes normalized requests and stores eligible successful JSON responses for reuse. Misses go upstream; expiry and bypass controls exist. Streaming misses are not stored.

A hit can avoid another upstream model call, but cannot make an old complaint current or transform one account into several. Metadata is ignored by default, so a new date only in metadata is not a reliable freshness control. Provide revised substantive source content or use an appropriate bypass. Other execution costs can remain. This repository is evidence of a public implementation, not proof of every production behavior or a benchmark for this workflow.

Plans and the decision boundary

Dovetail's checked pricing page lists Free at $0, with one project, one channel and basic chat/summarization, and Enterprise at custom pricing. Confirm requirements with the vendor rather than relying on older per-seat price lists or assuming every homepage feature is free. Current Dovetail pricing.

Super advertises wallet-based pay-as-you-go use and optional paid plans. No head-to-head cost, quality or time benchmark was run here. Compare the actual work: recurring evidence management, access requirements, source review and correction effort, not just whether both products can generate text.

Choose Dovetail when shared customer intelligence is the core requirement. Consider a bounded Super pilot when the missing work is broader research around a question and the data can be handled appropriately. If your team already gets a reliable, source-linked brief from its specialist platform, adding another tool may not help.
