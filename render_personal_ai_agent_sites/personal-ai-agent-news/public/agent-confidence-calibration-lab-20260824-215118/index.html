What to notice

Certainty is not accuracy.
High certainty with noisy outcomes creates an impressive-looking agent that cannot justify its own confidence.
Feedback closes the loop.
A small or delayed sample lets miscalibration hide. More observations make the estimate less theatrical.
Calibration is a contract.
When a system says 70%, operators should know what that number means before they let it act.
