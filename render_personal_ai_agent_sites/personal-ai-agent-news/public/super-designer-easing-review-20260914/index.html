Time and distance are different
Both markers travel the same distance over the same duration. At halfway through time, linear motion is halfway through distance. Ease-in begins slowly; ease-out approaches its destination slowly. A step curve changes position in jumps. None is universally better: choose according to the transition's purpose and the surrounding interaction.
