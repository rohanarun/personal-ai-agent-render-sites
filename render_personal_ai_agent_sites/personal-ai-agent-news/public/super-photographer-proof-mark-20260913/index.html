Photographer Proof Mark

How to mark a preview without overwriting your original image.

Published by Super Powers AI. Reviewed September 13, 2026. Local image operation, not an AI-generated edit.

Illustrative color-block image.

A preview, not protection

Use a photo you have permission to edit. The mark is drawn into a new PNG; your source file is not overwritten. A visible mark does not prevent copying or removal and is not proof of ownership.

Large images are reduced to at most 1600 pixels on their longest side. Small images are not enlarged. Text shrinks to fit the working image width. The preview is the export canvas, so inspect the resulting PNG before sharing it.

The browser decodes the image and renders the text locally. This is not a metadata-preserving archival export or a print-color workflow. Reloading resets the workspace; files are not uploaded by these controls.
