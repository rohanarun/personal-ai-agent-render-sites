Super Powers AI vs Fathom

The meeting ended.
Did the work happen?

For a team lead, an action item is a useful beginning. It is not the same as a completed deliverable. Choose tools around the missing step: meeting capture, a task handoff, or the work inside another approved system.

Published by Super Powers AI. Official documentation reviewed September 15, 2026. This is an editorial workflow comparison, not a hands-on benchmark. Examples are fictional; no Fathom-to-Super integration was tested.

Its Asana integration can send action items and highlights into projects, and its public developer platform offers APIs and webhooks. Define “done” before choosing the agent

These fictional cases separate a useful handoff from evidence of completion. A task-system integration may solve the handoff completely. Additional computer use only makes sense when there is an actual execution gap.

“Bring three venue options to the next meeting”

Research, not a reservation

Meeting handoff: create a task for the coordinator, preserving constraints such as location, capacity and date.

Possible Super pilot: research permitted public venue pages and prepare a comparison with source links, observation times, missing prices and availability caveats.

Accept when: the three options match the constraints and a reviewer can trace each substantive claim to its source. An inquiry may still be required to establish actual availability.

Not authorized: contacting a venue, placing a hold, accepting terms or paying a deposit. A research artifact does not prove a reservation.

“Update the project plan with the agreed milestones”

A draft change, then a verified write

Meeting handoff: identify the agreed milestones and flag any uncertain date or owner for confirmation.

Possible Super pilot: prepare a proposed change list against an approved project-plan snapshot. Prefer a native integration if it already handles the edit reliably.

Accept when: a reviewer approves the exact changes, an authorized workflow writes them, and the destination shows the intended values. If the plan changed in the meantime, reconcile before writing.

Not established by a task: that the plan was updated. A task marked complete is not a substitute for inspecting the affected record.

“Send the customer a recap and the revised proposal”

Internal preparation is not external delivery

Meeting handoff: record the commitment, intended recipient and owner. Confirm which proposal version was approved.

Possible Super pilot: draft an internal recap from approved notes and identify unresolved terms. Do not invent commitments missing from the notes.

Accept when: the authorized reviewer checks the recipient, text and attachment, and the sending system records the approved send. Delivery, receipt and agreement are separate outcomes.

Not authorized: sending an unreviewed draft or treating a draft attachment as the final commercial offer.

Make a small, explicit handoff

Do not give a general agent an entire meeting archive just because it is available. Supply the minimum approved context for the task: the commitment, accountable owner, relevant constraints, source reference and acceptance criteria. Follow your organization's recording, sharing and retention rules; check applicable requirements before capture.

Prepare the requested internal artifact from the approved notes and sources. Keep uncertain commitments separate. Stop and report missing access or conflicting instructions. Do not notify anyone, publish, purchase or change records. Return the artifact location, sources used, unresolved questions and the checks the reviewer still needs to perform.

This is a proposed pilot instruction, not a security control. Limit permissions in the connected systems where possible. A prompt cannot reliably turn a broadly privileged account into a read-only one.

Prefer the path already connected

Fathom's native Asana workflow may be sufficient for getting a commitment into the team's task system. For custom delivery, investigate its API and webhook options before assuming browser automation is required. The purpose of a Super pilot is to close a demonstrated gap, not duplicate a working connection.

Real cache technology, not completion evidence

The public computer-use-cache project implements an OpenAI-compatible proxy. It stores eligible successful JSON responses and serves matching canonical requests from cache; misses go upstream. The documentation includes expiry and bypass controls.

The reviewed implementation is inspectable. A cache hit can avoid an upstream model call, but response reuse does not execute a project-plan edit, send a recap or verify a reservation. The current contents of the destination still matter.

Keep changing task input in the request context. Do not omit a revised date or recipient to force a cache match. The existence of this software is evidence of an implementation, not proof of end-to-end savings, automatic workflow compatibility or successful completion of your meeting commitments.

Test one follow-through task

Choose a non-sensitive internal artifact and compare the same task with the current process. Include one missing-source case and one changed-destination case. Neither should produce a confident claim of completion.

  • Confirm the approved source notes and the exact commitment.
  • Assign an accountable reviewer and define what the artifact must contain.
  • Check whether a native integration already covers the work.
  • Restrict access and identify actions requiring separate approval.
  • Record artifact location, source references and unresolved issues.
  • Check the destination state before reporting an external action complete.
  • Measure corrections, review time and total cost, including failed attempts.

We have not run a measured Fathom-versus-Super trial. No time saving, accuracy advantage or price superiority is claimed. A pilot succeeds only if the complete reviewed workflow improves on the alternative you actually use.

Price the required workflow

Fathom currently lists a free plan with recordings and instant summaries. Its paid Team and Business offerings have a two-user minimum, and features vary: the pricing page places CRM field sync in Business. Check the selected billing period and current plan terms before comparing totals. Fathom pricing, checked September 15, 2026

For Super, include model and work-surface usage plus human review. Compare that cost with the specific Fathom plan and integrations needed. A free recording allowance and a cached model response are different units; neither alone prices a completed workflow. Super product and pricing information

Bottom line

Choose Fathom for the meeting record and supported handoffs. Evaluate Super for a clearly bounded research or artifact-preparation step beyond them. Keep approval and completion evidence explicit, regardless of which tool performs the work.
