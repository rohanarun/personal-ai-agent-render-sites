A fast answer can be the wrong answer.

Caching is a systems decision about equivalence. If the world, user state, permissions, or source documents can change, similarity alone is not enough to justify reuse.

Define invalidation signals alongside cache keys. A cache hit should mean “safe to reuse under this contract,” not merely “close to something we saw before.”
