A useful mental model

Discovery is part of authorization.

An agent cannot safely use what it cannot describe. A visible name without a reliable contract is an invitation to guess at side effects and arguments.

Find the tool

Know which capability is available and who exposed it.

Read the schema

Understand argument shape, limits, and side effects before planning.

Withhold uncertainty

When the contract does not fit the intent, keep the capability unavailable. A missing tool is safer than an invented interpretation of one.
