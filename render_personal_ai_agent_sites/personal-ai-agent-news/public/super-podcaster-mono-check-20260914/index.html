What changed in the mix?

The mono sample is (left + right) / 2. A mono input is copied unchanged. Equal channels retain their level; exactly opposite channels cancel. The opposite-polarity example deliberately produces silence in mono. It is a teaching signal, not a diagnosis of your microphone setup.

Compare at a comfortable listening level. A lower mono RMS can flag material worth reviewing, but RMS is not perceived loudness or LUFS. Different channels can contain intentionally different content. Do not automatically normalize, invert a channel or discard stereo information based on one number.

The decoder may resample to the browser audio context's rate. Export is a new mono, 16-bit PCM WAV at that rate, with sample values clipped to [-1, 1] before quantization. There is no dithering, mastering or metadata preservation. Keep your original recording.
