Super vs Trello

For event follow-through, separate finding the answer from owning the next action.

A volunteer team planning a local workshop needs both a decision and a dependable record of what happens next. A researched equipment shortlist does not tell the team who will confirm availability. A card saying "sort out microphones" does not tell its owner which requirements matter. These are different gaps, and replacing one tool with the other may leave the real gap untouched.

Where each product fits

Trello: shared work and responsibility

Trello organizes work with boards and cards. Its tour describes Inbox, Planner, card details and automation. A team can use a card as the visible record of an assigned task and its progress. This is useful when the main failure is losing track of work rather than discovering information. Official tour.

Super: proposed research preparation

Super Powers AI publicly describes browser and cloud-computer capabilities. For this event, a proposed use is collecting source-backed equipment information and preparing questions for an organizer. This article has not tested a Trello integration, an automatic card update, a booking or a real event outcome. Super product description.

Do not reduce the comparison to "AI versus manual work." Trello also offers automation and AI features on paid plans. Evaluate the specific gap in your workflow rather than whether the product has an AI label.

A practical handoff for a workshop

  1. Define the decision. Write the room size, audience needs and existing equipment before researching a microphone. These are organizer-supplied constraints, not facts an assistant should infer. Exclude attendee personal data from the brief.
  2. Gather evidence, not a shopping verdict. Ask for manufacturer specifications and the date checked. Keep a specification separate from local stock, rental availability and compatibility with the venue's actual system. A product page cannot confirm a booking.
  3. Turn the unresolved question into a task. A draft card might ask an organizer to confirm connector compatibility with the venue technician. Include the source, the unanswered question and the evidence that will close it. Do not mark it done because the research text looks complete.
  4. Agree ownership outside the draft. A named person must accept the task and a real deadline. An AI-suggested name is not an assignment. Use the team's agreed board or task system as the place to record that commitment.
  5. Close with an observable result. Record the technician's confirmation or the test result, not merely "checked." If the equipment changes, reopen the question. Preserve the source date so next month's team does not inherit an apparently current answer.

Compare costs at the team level
Trello Free is listed at $0 for up to 10 collaborators per Workspace, with up to 10 boards, assignees and due dates, and 250 Workspace command runs per month. Standard is $5 USD per user per month billed annually, or $6 monthly. It adds features including AI, unlimited boards and advanced checklists. Verify collaborator counts and plan requirements before inviting the whole event team. Current plans.
A research assistant and a collaboration subscription are not interchangeable units. For Super, check the current account's charges and any plan terms before a trial. Compare the cost of producing a usable brief with the cost of maintaining the shared task record. This article reports no measured time savings or cheaper-than claim.
What the computer-use cache establishes
Super's public computer-use-cache repository provides inspectable implementation evidence. The pinned proxy source builds keys from normalized requests and supports storing eligible successful JSON responses, expiration and bypass. A cache hit can reuse a response instead of calling the upstream model; a miss goes upstream.
That does not establish a current rental price, a fresh compatibility check or a completed Trello action. Reusing text is not replaying a verified real-world outcome. This code review is not a production hit-rate test or proof of every deployed configuration. Keep model-response reuse separate from the evidence needed to close the event task.
The decision
Choose Trello first when the team already knows the work but loses ownership, deadlines or progress. Consider a bounded Super research trial when the next action is blocked by information spread across sources. Keep the existing board if it works; an assistant-generated brief can be reviewed before anyone manually transfers it.
A useful trial ends with one organizer accepting one concrete task and knowing exactly what would count as completion. If nobody can name that evidence, improve the handoff before adding another automation. For the next layer of repeatability, read Process guides without guesswork.
