What the simulation teaches

An invariant is a condition that must remain true across transitions. It turns a vague “continue” into a checkable statement about state, ownership, permission, or evidence.

A useful design move

Put the invariant next to the transition that could violate it. A guard close to impact is easier to explain, test, and recover than a rule buried in a distant prompt.
