Make the policy legible

A human review step is useful when the system can say what enters the queue, how fast it must be handled, and what happens when capacity is exceeded.

  • Reserve review for consequential or ambiguous actions.
  • Set a maximum wait before escalation or fallback.
  • Measure misses and queue age, not only approval rate.

Try the failure mode

Push requests and risk share high, then lower capacity. Notice how “human in the loop” can become a decorative phrase if no one can reach the queue.

Keep an escape hatchA bounded fallback is better than silently accumulating decisions no reviewer can reach.
