Grounding is a relationship.

A source can be authoritative and still fail to support a particular sentence. The question is whether the evidence entails, qualifies, or merely surrounds the claim.

Make claims as specific as the evidence allows. When retrieval is partial, narrow the answer, show uncertainty, or ask for a better source instead of decorating a guess with links.
