    Measure discussion, not people

    Illustrative scenario: a workshop facilitator records an opening discussion, pauses for a break, and begins a decision round. Each recorded segment is a manually labeled interval, not a transcript or a measure of anyone's contribution. A short discussion is not automatically an effective one.

    Recording closes the current interval and leaves the clock paused for the next topic. Hidden-page time is excluded after the visibility-change pause. Browser suspension and device sleep can still affect timing; this is a facilitation aid, not an audited attendance, billing or safety clock.

    Labels and durations exist only in this page until downloaded. Export includes completed segments and a separate current-segment snapshot, so unfinished time is not silently presented as completed. Reset clears both after confirmation.
