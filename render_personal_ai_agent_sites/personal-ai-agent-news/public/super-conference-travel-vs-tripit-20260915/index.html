Different jobs, not an automatic winner

Start with TripIt if your main problem is organizing bookings you already made. Its documented flow accepts forwarded confirmation emails and builds an itinerary. TripIt Pro adds flight-status alerts, reminders and other travel features. TripIt overview · TripIt Pro.

Evaluate Super if your main problem is an unresolved task. For example: compare the official venue address with a hotel location, gather the organizer's arrival instructions, or draft questions about a missing reservation detail. Test one bounded task first. These are proposed uses of its computer-use capabilities, not a claim of a built-in TripIt integration.

Begin with the immovable commitment

For a conference speaker, that may be the organizer's required arrival time, not the time the public session starts. Gather the venue address, date, local timezone and any check-in or rehearsal instructions from the organizer. If those details conflict, make that the first unresolved question.

Then separate the transport and accommodation choices from the event commitment. A proposed hotel near the venue is not a hotel reservation. A flight search result is not a ticket. A forwarded email can be useful evidence, but a later change may supersede it. Preserve the provider reference and the latest status.

Ask for a plan that leaves assumptions visible: which times are confirmed, which transfer times are estimates, and which details still need an answer. Do not pretend an estimated transfer duration is a guarantee. This article does not recommend a specific flight, route, buffer or destination.

A useful first request for Super

Prepare a conference arrival brief from the organizer's official instructions and the confirmations I choose to provide. Identify the venue, local dates and unresolved conflicts. Keep options separate from reservations. Cite the sources. Do not book, cancel, send messages or change anything.

Super's public site describes access to cloud apps, browsers and sandboxes, plus MCP and desktop CLI entry points. Those are concrete product surfaces for a pilot. They do not prove access to your airline account or your employer's travel system. Super capabilities and setup.

Give the pilot a narrow scope

Use one conference and a small set of official sources. Provide only information needed for the task, through an appropriate private channel. Avoid passwords, passport numbers and payment credentials in free-text instructions. Follow your organization's travel and data-handling requirements.

Ask for evidence, not confident prose

The output should include each source, the time it was checked, and unresolved questions. A timezone conflict or missing confirmation belongs near the top. An unavailable website is a missing observation, not permission to invent an answer.

Review before any external action

Read the original terms before approving a purchase or cancellation. If you later authorize a transaction, specify the exact action and constraints. Record the result and provider reference before retrying an uncertain attempt; a retry can create a duplicate reservation.

What computer-use cache changes

Super publishes a Computer-Use Cache proxy that can return stored responses for matching requests and send misses upstream. Its documented controls include bypass and expiry; model, messages and most other request fields affect the key. The implementation is inspectable, but it is not a travel reservation system.

Repeated formatting or planning requests may be candidates for reuse when the actual request matches. New dates, screenshots or provider results can prevent a hit. Crucially, the outside world can change while a prompt stays the same. Fresh availability, current prices and booking status must not be inferred from an old cached answer.

A hit can avoid one upstream model request. It does not prove that a ticket exists, refresh a flight status or eliminate all execution costs. We have not measured savings for this use case. Keep the cache's technical behavior separate from promises about your trip. Implementation reference.

Compare costs against the job

TripIt offers a free itinerary product; its Pro page lists a standard price of $49 per year, with a promotional offer also visible when checked September 15, 2026. Confirm the actual checkout terms. TripIt · Pro pricing and features.

Super describes pay-as-you-go usage with optional device plans. That is not an equivalent annual itinerary subscription. Evaluate the cost of the specific planning task, including review and corrections, rather than declaring one product cheaper. Super pricing.

If TripIt already organizes your travel and supplies the alerts you need, keep it. If the hard part is gathering conference-specific information and identifying gaps, test Super on that part. Neither tool should replace official provider confirmation, required documents or your organization's approval process.
