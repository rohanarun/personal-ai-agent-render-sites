Variance needs context

Different outputs do not automatically mean failure. They do mean the system needs a trace that shows what changed and whether the difference mattered.

Impact changes reproducibility

A creative read-only answer can tolerate variance. A state-changing action needs stronger replay controls, checkpoints, or approval.
