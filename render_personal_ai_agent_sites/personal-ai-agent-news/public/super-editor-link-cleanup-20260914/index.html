Keep destination meaning intact

A newsletter editor may want to remove campaign tags from a public article while retaining its article ID and language. This is an illustrative editorial choice, not a rule that a particular parameter is disposable. Nothing is selected automatically.

Selections apply to a decoded, case-sensitive parameter name and remove every occurrence of that name. The native URLSearchParams deletion operation provides this behavior. The audit retains the original string, removed name/value pairs and the prepared result.

URL serialization may normalize escaping, spaces or the host. Even keeping every field does not promise byte-for-byte identity. Signed URLs can break after any normalization. Do not use this desk for signed download links, credentials or sensitive tokens. Removing a fragment may change in-page navigation or an application's route.

This desk does not fetch the destination, verify its content, detect all tracking, or establish that two URLs are equivalent. Review the output and test a public destination separately before sending a newsletter.
