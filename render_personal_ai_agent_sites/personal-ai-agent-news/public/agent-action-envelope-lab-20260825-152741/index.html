Boundaries work when they travel.

A target without a boundary invites scope drift. A boundary without evidence becomes arbitrary. A change without rollback turns a small request into an irreversible surprise. The envelope keeps those pieces legible at the same moment.

This is a compact teaching model. Production systems still need explicit authorization, provenance, rollback paths, and human review for consequential actions.
