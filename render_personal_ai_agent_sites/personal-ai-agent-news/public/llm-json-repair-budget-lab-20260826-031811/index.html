Retries are policy.
A retry is not free and it is not a guarantee. It adds another opportunity to get a usable shape while extending the time before a downstream tool can safely act.
When the budget is visible, teams can decide where strict validation belongs, what latency is acceptable, and when to stop repairing and ask for a human or a safer fallback.
