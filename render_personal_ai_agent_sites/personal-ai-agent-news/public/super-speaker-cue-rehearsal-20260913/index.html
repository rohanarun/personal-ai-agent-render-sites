Measure practice, not quality
The clock measures elapsed time while running, not speaking time. Reading silently, hesitating, and leaving the page running all count. Pause when you step away. The export takes a timing snapshot without stopping the rehearsal.
A long cue is a revision signal, not a diagnosis. Ask whether it contains multiple ideas, needs a clearer example, or deserves its own cue. Rehearse again after editing; this tool does not score delivery or predict audience response.
