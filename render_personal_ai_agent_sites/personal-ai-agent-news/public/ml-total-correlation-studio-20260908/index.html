A dependence measure for the whole set

TC = H(X) + H(Y) + H(Z) - H(X,Y,Z)
TC = KL(P(X,Y,Z) || P(X)P(Y)P(Z))
I(X;Y | Z) = H(X,Z) + H(Y,Z) - H(Z) - H(X,Y,Z)

Total correlation compares the joint distribution with the product of its marginals. It is nonnegative and vanishes precisely under mutual independence. Pairwise independence is weaker.

At the XOR preset, X and Y are independent fair bits and Z = X XOR Y. All pairwise information is zero, but TC is one bit. At the shared-bit preset, X = Y = Z, so TC is two bits while each pair shares one bit.

The mixture first chooses one of those mechanisms. Then an independent Bernoulli noise bit flips Z. A 50% flip removes Z's dependence, but any dependence between X and Y remains. These are exact finite distributions, not estimates from samples.

Matrix multiplication with ml-matrix applies the eight-state noise channel. Zero probabilities contribute zero to entropy. All logarithms are base 2.
