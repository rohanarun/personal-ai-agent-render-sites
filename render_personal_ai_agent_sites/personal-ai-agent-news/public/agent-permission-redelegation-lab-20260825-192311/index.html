Make re-delegation explicit

When work changes, carry forward only the minimum permission and ask for a new decision when the new step crosses a side-effect boundary.

  • Describe the changed task before requesting access.
  • Do not inherit permissions just because identity is shared.
  • Record who confirmed the expanded scope.

Try the trap

Raise task change and risk while keeping confirmation low. The handoff may look seamless, but it has become a new authorization event.

Continuity is not consentA familiar workflow can still contain a new side effect that needs a fresh decision.
