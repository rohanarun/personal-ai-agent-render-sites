Make scope visible

Grounded answers are not only about citing a source. They connect a specific claim to the part of the source that supports it and mark the rest as uncertain.

  • State the claim at the source’s resolution.
  • Separate direct support from inference.
  • Invite a fresh check when consequence rises.

Try the trap

Increase specificity while leaving source breadth low. The answer may sound precise while its support gets thinner.

Precision needs supportA narrower source cannot justify a wider conclusion just because the wording sounds confident.
