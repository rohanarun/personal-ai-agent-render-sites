Start with the decision you need to make

Imagine choosing a print supplier for a workshop. You need a delivery date, a proof-approval process and an all-in quote. One website lists turnaround time; another says nothing about shipping. The second vendor has not necessarily failed your deadline. Its answer is unknown.

A good shortlist preserves three states: supported yes, supported no and unresolved. Record the exact requirement, supporting passage or quote, source, date checked and follow-up owner. Keep a vendor's general marketing claim separate from a commitment for your specific order.

A practical research handoff
  1. Fix the scope. Supply the order quantity, destination, required arrival date and nonnegotiable constraints. Do not ask for the "best vendor" without defining fit.
  2. Ask for evidence, not a score. Request official pages or current quotes, exact supporting text and unresolved questions. Separate production time from shipping time.
  3. Resolve the critical gap. A public estimate is not a booking or a delivery guarantee. Ask the supplier to confirm the actual order before treating the deadline as satisfied.
  4. Preserve the handoff. Store accepted evidence with its checked date and owner in your chosen shared system. Keep stale quotes visible as stale; do not silently overwrite an earlier commitment.
Prerequisites: permission to use the source material, a named reviewer and a place to maintain decisions. Do not submit confidential quotes to any assistant without checking your organization's data policy. Research authority is not authority to contact suppliers, place orders or spend money.

What the computer-use cache does and does not prove
The public cache project is inspectable technology. In the pinned server implementation, normalized requests identify cached responses; metadata is ignored by default. A hit returns a stored response without that upstream model call. Misses go upstream; eligible JSON responses can be stored. TTL and bypass controls matter.
That is not evidence that a supplier's price or availability is still current. Reusing a model response does not itself re-open a website, verify a quote or authorize a purchase. Other compute and service costs remain. The public code is not an audit of every production setting.


Choose the missing capability
Already have dependable vendor data? Airtable may be sufficient. Still chasing evidence across sources? Evaluate a bounded Super research brief, with human review before a decision. For a one-off choice between two vendors, a plain document may be enough.
