What to notice

Context prevents duplicate work.
The handoff should include what was tried, what changed, and which evidence mattered.
Uncertainty protects judgment.
A human can decide better when the agent distinguishes known facts from open questions.
Next actions create momentum.
“I’m stuck” is not a handoff unless the next person knows what to verify or choose.
Try the failure case.
