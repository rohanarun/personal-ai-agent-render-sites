What to notice

Ambiguity multiplies candidates.
When a query can mean several things, relevance scores alone can hide a semantic mismatch.
Overlap weakens provenance.
Near-duplicate sources make a confident answer look supported even when every result repeats the same mistake.
Reranking needs a reason.
A second pass should reward intent fit and independent support, not just lexical similarity.
Try the failure case.
Push ambiguity and overlap high, then lower discipline. The answer may stay fluent while its evidence collapses.
