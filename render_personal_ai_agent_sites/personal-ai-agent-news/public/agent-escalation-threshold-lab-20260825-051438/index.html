Not every action deserves autonomy.

An agent should earn permission in proportion to what can go wrong. Tune consequence, reversibility, and confidence to find the right escalation path.

Act with a log

The action is reversible and the evidence is strong enough for bounded autonomy. Record what happened and make rollback visible.

Reversibility buys room

Preview, draft, and sandbox modes let an agent make progress while preserving a human's ability to inspect and undo the consequential step.

Confidence is not permission

Even a well-supported plan may need approval when the downside is large. Separate “likely correct” from “authorized to commit.”
