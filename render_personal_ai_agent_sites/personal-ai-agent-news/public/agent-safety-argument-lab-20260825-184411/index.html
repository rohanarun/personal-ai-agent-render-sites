What makes it defensible

A safety argument connects a claim to evidence, names what the evidence does not cover, and specifies a response when uncertainty matters.

  • Separate confidence from coverage.
  • Make the cost of a false positive visible.
  • Prefer reversible actions when the argument is incomplete.

Try the trap

Raise evidence strength while leaving coverage low. The agent may become more confident without becoming more justified.

Coverage is the questionA strong source can still be irrelevant to the full claim. Ask what part of the decision it actually supports.
