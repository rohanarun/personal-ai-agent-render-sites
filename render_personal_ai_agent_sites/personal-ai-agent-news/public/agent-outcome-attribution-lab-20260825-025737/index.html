What the simulation teaches

Attribution is a data contract between action and outcome. Without shared IDs, clocks, and enough context, a plausible story can look like evidence.

A useful design move

Record the action, observation time, outcome, and relevant alternatives together. When the signal is delayed or noisy, preserve uncertainty instead of rewarding the last action automatically.
