Super vs Doodle

A time everyone likes is not yet a rehearsal ready to run.

Your panelists agree on Tuesday afternoon. The moderator has a different time zone in mind, the video link belongs to last year's event, and nobody has checked who will admit the guests. The scheduling problem is partly solved. The coordination problem is not.

Let the scheduling tool collect availability

Doodle's Group Poll lets an organizer offer candidate times and see responses together. Its product page says participants can respond without an account, describes response tracking, and supports meeting details such as an agenda and location. This is a direct fit when your problem is finding a shared time. Official Group Poll overview.

Do not confuse the poll with a dry run of your meeting technology. The existence of a response does not establish that a speaker can share slides, join from the intended account or hear the moderator. Those are separate checks for the organizer.

Where Super could help an organizer

Super describes browsers, cloud sandboxes and automated research on its public site. A proposed use case is to read authorized event notes, identify missing rehearsal requirements and prepare an organizer's brief. It could be evaluated for comparing the event agenda with the rehearsal agenda, or separating public speaker information from details that still need confirmation. Super product description.

This is not a tested Doodle integration, live calendar read or automatic booking workflow. It also does not claim Super is better at polling. If a shared time is the only missing piece, Doodle alone may be enough. The case for another assistant is the work around the time, not duplicating the poll.

A useful preparation sequence

  1. Define who must attend. Separate required panelists and moderator from optional observers. A majority preference is not sufficient when a required speaker cannot join. Decide whether a partial technical check is acceptable before offering times.
  2. State the full time context. Include calendar date, intended time zone and duration. Avoid “Tuesday afternoon” or an ambiguous abbreviation as the final instruction. Have participants check the displayed event in their own calendar, especially around daylight-saving changes.
  3. Prepare a bounded brief. A proposed Super request: “From these approved event notes, draft a rehearsal checklist with participant roles, duration, agenda, host responsibilities and unresolved details. Keep confirmed facts separate from assumptions. Do not email anyone or book a time.”
  4. Collect responses, then finalize deliberately. Use the scheduling workflow to choose a time and confirm recipients. Verify the resulting event details and meeting access. Assign an owner for sending any corrections; do not silently edit a shared plan based on an assistant's inference.

A practical rehearsal agenda might cover introductions, microphone checks, slide ownership and the moderator's handoff cues. These are suggestions, not requirements for every event. Keep private access credentials out of public briefs, and share only information the recipients need.

A cached answer cannot confirm a calendar

Super publishes Computer-Use Cache. The reviewed implementation hashes normalized requests and can replay eligible successful JSON responses. A cache miss goes upstream; expiry and bypass controls exist. Streaming misses are not stored.

This is inspectable technology for response reuse, not evidence of fresh participant availability. A hit can avoid another upstream model call, while other execution costs can remain. Metadata is ignored by default, so a new date only in metadata does not reliably force a fresh answer. Updated substantive source content or an appropriate bypass is needed when freshness matters. The public proxy alone does not establish every deployed cache behavior.

For this workflow, an old coordination summary must never be treated as a new RSVP. Preserve the original observation time and recheck the authoritative event or participant response before saying the rehearsal is confirmed.
