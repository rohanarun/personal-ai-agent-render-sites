Progress can be small and still be useful.

When the best-looking action is hard to undo, an agent can often decompose it. A smaller step preserves information and keeps the next decision available.

A working rule
Prefer the action that preserves the next choice.

Compare upside, downside, and reversibility. When uncertain, buy information with a bounded step instead of spending all authority at once.
