Boundaries are reviewable

A translator preparing public museum copy may want each proposed sentence as a separate review unit. The browser's Intl.Segmenter supplies locale-sensitive sentence boundaries. It is not a translation model or a guarantee of the editorial boundaries you intended.

Merge a unit with the next when they belong together; undo reverses the latest merge. Every unit remains an exact contiguous slice of the input, including whitespace. Export records start and end offsets in JavaScript UTF-16 code units, with the end excluded. Those offsets are not byte positions or visible-character counts.

Re-segmenting discards manual merges. Editing the source or changing language clears previous results so offsets cannot silently refer to an older passage. Results may vary with browser and locale data; the resolved locale is saved. This desk merges boundaries but does not insert new ones or align translated text.

Keep the review boundary clear

Use a passage you are permitted to process. The fixture does not certify fluency, completeness or accessibility. Compare the source units to your final translation separately, and keep a human reviewer responsible for publication.
