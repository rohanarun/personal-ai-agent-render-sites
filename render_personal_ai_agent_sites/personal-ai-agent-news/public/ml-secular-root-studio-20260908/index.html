A small update, a structured eigenproblem

For A = D + rho u u transpose, the updated eigenvalues away from D's diagonal entries solve the secular equation. Here D = diag(1,2,4), rho is positive, and u has unit length with all coordinates nonzero.

f(x) = 1 + rho sum_i u[i]^2 / (d[i] - x)
u[i]^2 = [w, (1-w)/2, (1-w)/2]
1 < root1 < 2 < root2 < 4 < root3 <= 4 + rho

Between poles, f increases because its derivative is a sum of positive squared-denominator terms. Its jumps therefore bracket the roots. The largest eigenvalue stays below 4 + rho because u has unit length.

ml-matrix computes the eigenvalues directly. Their secular residuals independently check the rational equation. The total eigenvalue shift equals rho, the trace of the rank-one update. The positive controls avoid zero-coordinate deflation and zero-strength boundary cases.
