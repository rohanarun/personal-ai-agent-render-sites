A citation is not evidence

Zotero vs Super Powers AI for a literature review you can trace back to the papers.

A real DOI and a correctly formatted reference do not establish that a paper supports your sentence. Keep two jobs separate: managing the source record, and checking what the source actually says. An assistant can help with a bounded reading workflow, but it does not remove that distinction.

Build the library before the argument

Start a course review with a question narrow enough to investigate. “Does this intervention help?” needs a population, outcome and context before a pile of search results becomes useful. Record the databases or search services used, the queries and dates, and why a candidate belongs in the review. This is a practical course-work routine, not a claim to a complete systematic-review protocol.

Save the bibliographic item, then check its title, authors, year and identifier against the source. Keep a preprint and a later published version distinguishable. A PDF filename is not dependable metadata. Add a short note on relevance before you forget why you saved it.

Zotero's guide describes collections, tags, attached notes and files, browser capture, and adding items by identifiers such as DOI or ISBN. The same item can belong to more than one collection without becoming separate copies. Those are useful foundations for a review you will revise over time. Zotero quick-start guide.

Do not promote a search hit into a finding

Imagine a fictional paper about a classroom intervention. Its title sounds relevant, the abstract mentions an improvement, and a later results table reports only a short-term outcome in one setting. A broad claim about all students would outrun that evidence. This example is deliberately not a fabricated real citation.

At title level, record a candidate and a reason to inspect it. At abstract level, label the note as abstract-only and avoid implying you checked the methods or tables. With full text, record the specific location, study context, limitations and the claim you think it supports. Having opened the PDF is not enough; the passage still has to support the claim.

Keep the evidence boundary in the note

This local worksheet does not retrieve papers, validate citations or generate a scholarly conclusion. Your text stays in page memory; the exported note remains a draft for review.



Where Zotero fits best
Choose Zotero for collecting, organizing, annotating and citing research. Its official product supports bibliographies and integration with Word, LibreOffice and Google Docs. It is free and open source. If your problem is inconsistent references or losing track of PDFs, start there rather than replacing a reference manager with generated prose. Zotero's product overview.
Online file storage is a separate consideration. Zotero lists 300 MB free, 2 GB for $20/year, 6 GB for $60/year and unlimited storage for $120/year, in USD. These plans concern synchronized attachments, not a per-citation fee. Group file storage draws from the group owner's account. Check current terms and institutional options before buying. Where a Super reading brief might help
Super's public product describes browser-based research and cloud-computer capabilities. A proposed use case is a source-linked reading brief: gather permitted public source pages, distinguish accessible full text from abstracts, and return questions or comparisons for a student to verify. This article has not tested a Zotero connector, library sync or automatic citation export. Super Powers AI.
Supply the scope and acceptable sources. Ask for missing information to stay missing, and require source locations for claims. Do not request invented references to fill a quota. Follow your course's AI-use rules, respect access restrictions, and do not upload restricted or confidential material without permission. An assistant should help inspect an argument, not impersonate reading you have not done.


Cache technology does not validate a paper
The public computer-use-cache repository provides inspectable code. Its pinned server implementation hashes normalized requests and can reuse eligible successful JSON responses. Misses go upstream; expiry and bypass controls govern reuse.
A cache hit can avoid an upstream model call. It cannot establish that a claim is true, that a paper has not been corrected, or that a passage supports a conclusion. Recheck source versions where relevant. The code proves a response-reuse mechanism, not measured savings for this reading workflow or every production setting.


A small trial with a clear standard
Try three papers you can legally access and have time to check. Compare the brief with each original source. Count unsupported claims, wrong metadata and missing caveats; track correction effort. Keep Zotero as the source library and adopt an additional assistant only if the reviewed result helps. A longer bibliography is not evidence of a better review.
