Protect the edge

The right control is often one more check, one narrower action, or one explicit refusal to cross a thin margin.

Proceed inside the margin

Act when confidence is strong, consequences are bounded, and residual uncertainty cannot create an outsized surprise.

Buy one more fact

Verify the hinge assumption when a small amount of evidence can widen the space between plan and harm.

Refuse the thin edge

Stop or escalate when consequence severity and uncertainty leave no credible recovery room.

This simulation is a teaching model, not a production safety policy. It demonstrates a contract-first pattern: measure confidence, consequence, uncertainty, and recovery space together.
