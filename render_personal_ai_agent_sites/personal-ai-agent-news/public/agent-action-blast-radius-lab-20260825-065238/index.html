Four ways to reduce blast radius.

The core move is to change the action, not just the narration around it.

Preview the diff

Show what will change in a human-readable form before a write, send, or deletion crosses the boundary.

Prefer a narrow pilot

Start with one record, one recipient, or one environment when the action can reveal information safely.

Leave a checkpoint

Persist enough state to explain, pause, or reverse the move without reconstructing the whole world.
