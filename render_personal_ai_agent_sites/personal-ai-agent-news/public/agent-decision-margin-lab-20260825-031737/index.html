What the simulation teaches

Decision thresholds should depend on consequences. A low-impact draft can tolerate ambiguity that an irreversible action cannot.

A useful design move

Make the margin explicit: continue, gather evidence, ask for clarification, or route to a human when confidence does not clear the cost of being wrong.
