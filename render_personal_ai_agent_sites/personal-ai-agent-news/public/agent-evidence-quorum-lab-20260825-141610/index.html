Claim outcomes

The agent should preserve the boundary between what the evidence shows and what it merely suggests.

PostureEvidenceAgent action
ASSERTIndependent agreement is strong and claim risk is bounded.State the result with linked provenance.
QUALIFYThe sources align, but the claim still needs a careful boundary.Use scoped language and name uncertainty.
WITHHOLDAgreement or provenance cannot carry the claim risk.Do not convert the evidence into a conclusion.
A useful mental model

Confidence should shrink when the claim grows.

A high-stakes claim needs stronger, more independent support than a low-stakes observation. Quorum is a way to keep language proportional to evidence.

Compare sources

Look for independent agreement, not repeated copies of one source.

Price the claim

Raise the evidence threshold when an error would cause meaningful harm.

Keep the boundary

A qualified result is useful when it makes uncertainty legible. Withholding is useful when the evidence cannot safely carry the conclusion.
