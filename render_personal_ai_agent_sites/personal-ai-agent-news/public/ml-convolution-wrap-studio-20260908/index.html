Multiplication needs room

Two sequences of lengths 4 and 3 have a full linear convolution of length 4 + 3 - 1 = 6. An FFT length at least 6 prevents tail wraparound. This library uses powers of two, so the padded option is 8.

linear[k] = sum x[i] h[k-i]
circular[k] = sum linear[k + m N]

Outside the input ranges, x and h are zero. The second equation folds all valid linear indices modulo N. For N = 4, indices 4 and 5 add into indices 0 and 1. For N = 8, the six linear samples remain intact and two trailing zeros remain.

The NumPy convolution documentation explains the full output length and the padding needed when multiplying in the Fourier domain. This page uses fft.js for the transforms and a direct sum as an independent check.

This is mathematical convolution, with kernel reversal implicit in h[k-i], not the cross-correlation operation often called convolution in neural-network libraries. It is a numerical demonstration, not a speed benchmark. Tiny imaginary remainders are floating-point error; export preserves them.
