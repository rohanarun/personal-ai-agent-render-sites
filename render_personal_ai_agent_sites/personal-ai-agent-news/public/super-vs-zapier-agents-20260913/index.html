Start with the work and the accounts it touches. A connected business workflow and a personal cloud-app task are different buying decisions, even when both products use agents.

Zapier Agents

Zapier documents agents that connect company knowledge and perform work across its app ecosystem. Its product page includes activity monitoring, web work, and templates for lead research and support tasks.

Evaluate first when: your main requirement is work across existing business-app connections. This is our fit judgment, not a measured win. Check the exact action and account permissions before committing.

What Super's cache does and does not prove

Super's product demonstration; not a comparative test.

The inspected local-execution workflow-cache implementation returns a workflow and verification evidence on a match, or an explicit miss when no safe workflow matches. The caller must handle a miss; the lookup does not itself execute a fallback. This is concrete implementation evidence, not proof that every cloud-app path uses that route.

No shared-task benchmark, hit-rate measurement, cost comparison, or reliability ranking was performed here. A cache hit is not a guarantee that an external app has stayed unchanged. We make no claim about Zapier's internal caching.

Before switching

Run the same non-sensitive task with the same success criteria in both products. Record setup effort, output evidence, approval behavior, failures, and the total charge you actually observe. Keep your existing workflow until the replacement passes your required checks.

Prices and allowances vary by plan and usage. This page does not quote a price or claim either product is cheaper. Check each provider's current offer and the cost of the exact task.
