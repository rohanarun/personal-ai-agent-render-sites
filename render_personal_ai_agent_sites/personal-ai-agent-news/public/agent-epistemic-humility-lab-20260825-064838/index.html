Humility is not passivity. It is a routing decision: preserve the user's momentum while making uncertainty visible.

Four habits of a humble agent.

Good orchestration turns uncertainty into a visible control surface instead of burying it inside a fluent answer.

Separate claim from action

A model can phrase a plausible claim while an action still needs authorization, verification, or a smaller reversible step.

Ask when intent branches

Clarification is valuable when different interpretations would lead to materially different tools, recipients, or outcomes.

Abstain with a path forward

“I cannot establish that yet” becomes useful when paired with the exact missing evidence or a safe next check.
