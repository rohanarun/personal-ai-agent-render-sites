Your task signal is doing the heavy lifting. Keep the answer-bearing facts explicit, then trim history before adding more retrieval.

1. Name the signal

Start with the instruction and evidence that directly determine the answer. Everything else is competing for attention.

2. Spend history carefully

Continuity is useful until stale turns dilute the active task. Summaries preserve shape without keeping every word.

3. Retrieve with a filter

More documents do not automatically mean more truth. Relevance is a quality gate, not a volume target.
