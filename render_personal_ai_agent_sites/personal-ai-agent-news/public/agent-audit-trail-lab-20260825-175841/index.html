Trust needs a trail.

A decision is easier to review when the system can show what it saw, who authorized it, what changed, and how to undo it. Tune those four parts of an agent audit trail to see whether the action is accountable.

Audit posture

Proceed with a reviewable trail

auditability = min(observation, authority, change, undo)

Why the minimum?

A detailed observation does not compensate for missing attribution. A clear actor does not help if nobody can tell what changed. The minimum keeps one invisible gap from being mistaken for a complete history.
