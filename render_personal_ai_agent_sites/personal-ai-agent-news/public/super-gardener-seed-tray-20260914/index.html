Make the physical record unambiguous

Put an A1 marker on the real tray and keep the same orientation in your photos. A correct digital label is not useful if the tray is rotated without notice. Keep seed-packet instructions alongside your record; this page does not estimate germination, spacing, frost dates or harvest windows.

A blank date means no date was recorded, not that the cell is unsown. A label count is not a seedling count. Observation notes can preserve distinctions such as “label replaced” or “not checked” without pretending they are measurements.

Actual procedure reuse, with a boundary

First-party demonstration, not a test of a gardening journal.

This is procedure reuse, not a cache of your plant-care advice. Missing context, no stored entries or no safe match returns a cache miss. The calling system must handle that miss; the route alone does not prove an automatic fallback. Matching can incur model charges. Reusing an eligible procedure can avoid constructing it again, but live eligibility, time savings and task costs were not measured for this use case.

A changed journal layout, new permissions or a different destination deserves fresh verification. Never interpret an old successful save as proof that today's tray record was written. Review Super's current product and wallet/device plans.
