Penalty is not a ban.

A decoder can reduce the score of tokens found in the generated context without deleting them from the vocabulary. The exact implementation varies, but the learning idea is stable: history becomes a signal that shifts the competition. Gentle penalties preserve coherence; aggressive ones can force awkward novelty.
