Choose the execution model

Pipedream: own the workflow

Pipedream documents event-triggered sequences of steps, prebuilt actions and custom code. Its workflow inspector exposes execution details. Prefer this model when you need to connect APIs with an explicit trigger and maintain the integration yourself.

Pipedream workflow documentation

Super: delegate the task

Super presents a task-oriented assistant with cloud apps, browser execution and interactive tool creation. Consider it when your desired outcome involves app actions rather than only passing an event between APIs. Verify the exact task, account permissions and completion evidence before relying on it.

Super capabilities and current plans

Our assessment, not a benchmark: Pipedream is the clearer fit for developer-owned event pipelines. Super is worth evaluating for delegated app work. Neither automatically replaces the other; this page does not establish a price, reliability or speed winner.

Make the handoff explicit

Before connecting either system, decide which event fields are necessary, who can authorize the action, and what output proves completion. An exported field inventory is a planning artifact, not authorization to execute.
