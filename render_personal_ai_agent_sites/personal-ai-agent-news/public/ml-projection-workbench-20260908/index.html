What PCA optimizes

The first principal component captures the largest variance of centered data. For an orthogonal projection onto one line through the mean, this is also the direction with the smallest sum of squared residual distances. It is not necessarily the best feature for predicting a label.

This lab subtracts each feature's mean but does not standardize its scale. All values are synthetic and use the same units. Rescaling a feature can change the result.

The exact calculation

For centered point z and unit direction u, the retained coordinate is t = z dot u. Reconstruction is t times u. The displayed error is the mean of squared Euclidean residual lengths, not the mean over individual coordinates.

Retained variance = 1 - error / mean squared centered radius. When every point is identical, that denominator is zero, so the fraction is undefined. Equal eigenvalues make the optimal direction non-unique.
