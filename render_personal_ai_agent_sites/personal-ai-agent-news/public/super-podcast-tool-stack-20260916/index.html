Choose your podcast tools

Super Powers AI, Riverside and Descript solve overlapping but different jobs. Start with the bottleneck in your next episode, not a universal “best app” ranking.

For an independent interview show, the useful distinction is between gathering evidence, capturing the conversation and shaping the recording. You may need one production platform plus a research assistant, not three subscriptions.

Where each belongs

Super Powers AI

Consider for preparation across sources. Its public product describes an assistant with cloud apps, browser and sandboxes. An appropriate trial is a linked guest brief or a question outline assembled from approved public material.

This is a proposed podcast workflow, not a tested native Riverside or Descript integration. We have not verified multitrack recording, professional media editing or podcast hosting in Super. Keep the recording platform when those are your primary needs. Product evidence.

Riverside

Consider for a recording-to-release workflow. Riverside presents recording, text-based editing, AI repurposing, streaming and publishing together. It is not merely a remote recorder. Choose it when keeping production stages together matters; test your guest setup and destination before committing.

Don't add a second editor automatically. First see whether Riverside's own editing meets your needs. Its official overview is the capability source, not a benchmark of output quality.

Descript

Consider for transcript-led revision. Descript edits audio and video through text, offers Underlord assistance, and also records guests through Rooms. It can cover capture as well as editing; “Riverside records, Descript edits” is too simplistic.

Choose it when rearranging a conversation through its transcript is central to your process. Audition edits against the original recording, especially names, pauses and altered context. Official podcast workflow.

Pick the next episode's bottleneck

Editorial decision guide. No accounts connect, no media uploads, and no AI request runs here.

A handoff that survives the edit

Imagine a 30-minute interview with a local-business founder. Before recording, collect their public biography, one recent announcement and the original source for any numerical claim. Mark a revenue figure as unconfirmed when only a third-party article mentions it. Ask a question instead of converting that figure into a scripted fact.

Illustrative brief: “Your announcement mentions a new location. What changed in the operating model? Please confirm the opening date. The revenue figure in the secondary article is not verified and should not appear in the introduction.”

Get recording consent and confirm pronunciations. Record a short sample in the chosen production platform. After the interview, keep a timestamp for every cut affecting meaning. Check the final introduction against the approved brief, not against an early AI draft. A producer, not a cached answer, owns the final approval.

This example is authored guidance, not a customer outcome. Use approved accounts and non-sensitive sources for the first trial; verify each service's retention and permission settings before sharing unreleased material.

What the computer-use cache actually does

Super's public Computer-Use Cache is inspectable technology: an OpenAI-compatible proxy. In the pinned implementation, a matching normalized request can reuse a stored response. A miss goes upstream; bypass controls are available. Streaming misses are not stored on this path.

A hit avoids another upstream model call. It does not re-check a guest's biography, record an interview, approve a quotation or remove other execution costs. Changed source material needs fresh verification. This code review is not proof of every production configuration or a measured speed advantage over either competitor.

Budget for the job, not the logo

Descript's plans distinguish media hours, AI credits and export capabilities. Its Free tier allows a trial; paid-plan limits matter for repeated production. Riverside's pricing page includes a Free option, but the retrieved page contained inconsistent plan details and a staging warning, so we do not quote a paid price here. Confirm the live checkout, billing period and limits.

Super advertises a pay-as-you-go wallet and separate managed-device offerings. That is not a per-episode quote. Run one approved, bounded research task and inspect its actual usage before projecting a monthly cost. No evidence here establishes the cheapest option.

Make one episode the trial

Keep your existing production tool until the alternative passes your own sample: guest access, recording recovery, transcript corrections, final export and approval. Add Super only if the cross-source preparation job remains painful. Compare the finished handoff, not a feature count.
