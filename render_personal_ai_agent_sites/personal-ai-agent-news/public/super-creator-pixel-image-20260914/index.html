Creator Pixel Image

How to make a blocky image treatment without rebuilding the artwork one square at a time.

PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 8 MiB and 16 megapixels. The long edge of the exported image is at most 1024 pixels. Animated inputs use one decoded frame.

Fewer samples, larger blocks

The image is drawn into a small grid, then enlarged with canvas image smoothing disabled. The slider changes the grid's longer edge; the shorter edge is rounded to a whole pixel. The PNG preserves the exact dimensions shown below the preview. Extreme aspect ratios can shift slightly because the grid cannot have fractional pixels.

The sample is original geometric artwork. Your chosen file is processed by this page's browser code; this tool does not send that file to an AI model or a server. The product video below is hosted externally.

Visual styling, not secure redaction. Do not use pixelation to conceal names, faces, account numbers or confidential content. Details may remain recognizable. Remove sensitive material from the source before sharing.

Why the export can look different from the original

Downsampling discards detail and uses the browser's image resampling. This is not palette quantization, vector tracing, or a trained pixel-art model. Transparency is retained where the browser preserves it. The displayed image may be scaled by your screen; the downloaded PNG contains the full output pixels. Color handling can vary between browsers.
