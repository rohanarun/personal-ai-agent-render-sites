An uncertainty budget simulator

Every workflow has a limit for what can stay unknown.

An agent can carry a small ambiguity through a reversible draft. The same ambiguity becomes unacceptable when the next move is durable. Tune the budget and see when clarification becomes the responsible action.

Uncertainty is cheap only before commitment.

A good agent does not aim for zero ambiguity everywhere. It spends clarification effort where uncertainty meets an irreversible or high-consequence action.

Carry what is reversible

Drafts, previews, and simulations can preserve momentum while making uncertainty visible.

Stop at the commitment edge

Before sending, mutating, granting, or publishing, require the missing fact that changes the action.

Ask the smallest useful question

Clarification should collapse the decision space, not ask a human to repeat the entire investigation.
