What to notice

Independence beats repetition.
Many copies of one claim do not create the same support as separate paths to the same conclusion.
Stakes raise the bar.
A high-consequence decision may need more evidence even when the answer feels obvious.
Search has diminishing returns.
When new sources add no useful information, continuing can add noise rather than confidence.
Try the failure case.
Push independence and information gain low while stakes are high. The agent should keep looking or defer.
