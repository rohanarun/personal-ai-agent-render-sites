A risk routing simulator

Escalation is a risk decision, not a panic button.

An agent should route work based on what could go wrong and how cleanly it can recover. Tune uncertainty, consequence, and reversibility to see the right operating mode.

Escalate before publishing.

The unresolved exception meets a high-consequence, low-reversibility action. A fast publication would turn a known gap into a broad side effect.

Recommended posture: hold publication, state the missing exception, and request a focused review.

Route the risk, not just the task.

Escalation is most useful when it preserves momentum and control: continue low-risk preparation, review decisions that need judgment, and stop before a harmful commitment.

Uncertainty is not uniform

Ask whether the unknown changes the action, not merely whether any unknown exists.

Consequence sets the bar

High-impact actions need stronger evidence and clearer authority than reversible internal work.

Make review narrow

A good escalation names the exact exception and decision, so a reviewer can unblock the smallest possible step.
