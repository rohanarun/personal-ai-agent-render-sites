What to notice

Expiry is a safety feature.

Approvals should carry enough context to act and enough limits to stop stale authority from becoming invisible.

Impact

High-impact actions need shorter windows.

A reviewer can approve a draft without approving every later mutation. Scale the deadline to the consequence of being wrong.

Latency

Time changes the state under review.

When the reviewer responds late, the agent should re-check the facts and approval scope instead of treating delay as permission.

Escalation

Expired does not mean abandoned.

A good policy hands off with the request, current state, deadline, and missing decision so a reviewer can resolve it quickly.

Evidence

Keep approval and action linked.

Record what was approved, when, under which constraints, and what the agent eventually did. That makes the boundary auditable.
