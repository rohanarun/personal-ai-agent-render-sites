    A preview is not a transcript check

    The browser's native text-track parser reads this draft. WebVTT represents timed cues for captions, subtitles, and related uses. A loaded track may still omit malformed cues; a parsed cue count is not complete format validation.

    Illustrative workflow: export a browser-playable video and its WebVTT draft from your editor, load the video here, and paste the draft. Compare each cue against the audio and visual context. Recheck names, speaker changes, missing speech, timing, and line breaks in your actual publishing platform.

    This page does not transcribe, translate, edit timestamps, burn captions into video, or certify accessibility. Browser codecs and text-track rendering vary. Local video selection uses a blob URL; the page has no upload or model-call code. The default demo is an external network request. Reloading discards the draft and selection.
