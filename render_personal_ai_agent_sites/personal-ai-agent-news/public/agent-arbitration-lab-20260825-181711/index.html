Arbitration posture

Resolve with the rule visible

resolution quality = min(evidence, authority, safety, tie)

Why the minimum?

A strong fact pattern cannot replace unclear authority. A safe action is not enough if nobody can explain why it won. The minimum keeps conflict resolution inspectable and makes escalation a valid result.
