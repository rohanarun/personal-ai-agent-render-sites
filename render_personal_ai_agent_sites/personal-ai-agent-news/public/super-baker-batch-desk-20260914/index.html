Baker's Batch Desk

Scale ingredient quantities without turning thirds into rounded decimals.

A quantity handoff, not a new recipe
The initial ingredient list is an illustrative arithmetic example, not a tested recipe. Each amount is multiplied by target yield divided by original yield. Names and units remain exactly what you enter. Fractions such as 1/3 and mixed quantities such as 1 1/2 are accepted by Fraction.js, the bundled rational-arithmetic library.
Keep a copy of the original recipe and compare every ingredient before using the result. A fractional egg count is an arithmetic result, not a recommendation for how to measure eggs. The desk does not convert cups to grams, adjust baking time or temperature, choose pan sizes, account for ingredient behavior, or validate allergens. Recipe performance does not follow from multiplication alone.
Yield and amounts must be positive. Up to 30 ingredients are supported. Edits invalidate the export until the batch is scaled again. The download preserves original and target yield, exact multiplier, original amounts, units and scaled quantities for review. Nothing here orders ingredients or sends recipe data to an AI service.
