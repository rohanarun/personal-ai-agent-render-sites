A decision is not just a task
Separate the choice from the follow-up. "Ask for a quote" is an action; "Choose the venue after reviewing two quotes" is still an open decision. Use Deferred when the group intentionally postpones a choice, and record what would unblock it.
Illustrative scenario: a small team agrees on a workshop format but has not chosen the venue. Keep the format as Decided and the venue as Open. Confirm owners and dates with the people involved before distributing the brief. Avoid confidential, employee, client, or other sensitive details unless your organization's rules permit handling them here and in any exported file.
