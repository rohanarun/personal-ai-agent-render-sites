The first symbol costs more than the next

For a stationary first-order Markov chain, entropy rate is conditional entropy H(X next | X current). Each later symbol contributes the same conditional entropy, so the chain rule gives the exact block formula.

h = sum_i pi[i] H(P[i,:])
H(X1,...,Xn) = H(pi) + (n-1)h
H(block)/n = h + (H(pi)-h)/n

Conditioning cannot increase entropy: h is at most H(pi). Independent symbols make the two equal. Persistent and alternating chains can both have low entropy rates despite balanced marginals.

ml-matrix solves the stationary balance equations. The numerical check enumerates every binary path at the selected length, up to 1,024 paths, and compares its entropy with the chain-rule formula. The positive transition range ensures a unique stationary distribution. Samples use browser randomness and are illustrative, not entropy estimates.
