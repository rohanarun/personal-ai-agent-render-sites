Because Q is orthogonal, RQ = QᵀAkQ. Each step is an orthogonal similarity transformation, preserving eigenvalues, trace, and Frobenius norm. Z accumulates the Q factors; it connects the current matrix to the original one.

For these symmetric examples, a diagonal limit would reveal the eigenvalues. The original eigenvalues shown above are computed separately, not guessed from the current diagonal.

The tied-magnitude example contains [[0,1],[1,0]], whose eigenvalues are -1 and 1. At zero offset its off-diagonal norm remains unchanged: preservation is not convergence. A nonzero offset breaks that exact symmetry.

This experiment uses ml-matrix's Householder QR factorization without shifts or deflation. Practical eigensolvers use more sophisticated steps for speed and robustness. The timeline ends at 30 iterations, not at a claimed universal convergence threshold.
