Two knobs, two jobs.

Temperature changes the softness of the whole distribution by dividing logits before softmax. Top-p is a second filter: sort candidates, keep the shortest prefix that reaches the chosen mass, and renormalize it. The result is not “random versus deterministic”; it is a visible trade between variety and a controlled frontier.

Why nucleus sampling?

Holtzman and colleagues introduced nucleus sampling to avoid low-probability degeneration while preserving natural variation.
