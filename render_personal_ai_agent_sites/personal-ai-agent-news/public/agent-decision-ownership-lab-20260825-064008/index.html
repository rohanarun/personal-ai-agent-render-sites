What to notice

Make responsibility legible.

An agent can do the work without quietly becoming the owner of the decision.

Recommendation

Prepare evidence and options.

A strong agent can summarize the state, surface tradeoffs, and propose a next action without pretending the user's choice has already been made.

Approval

Put the decision at the boundary.

High-impact actions should show what is about to happen, under which constraints, and who is accepting the consequence.

Execution

Record who authorized the action.

Keep recommendation, approval, tool call, and result connected so later review can distinguish assistance from ownership.

Handoff

Unclear ownership should pause.

If a policy cannot tell who decides, the agent should stop with a compact handoff instead of letting ambiguity become silent automation.
