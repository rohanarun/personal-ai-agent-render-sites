Use the chain with a visible checkpoint

chain strength = min(source, transform, identity, link)

Why the minimum?

A strong source cannot rescue an untraceable transformation. Clear identity does not show whether the action followed from the evidence. The minimum keeps one broken link from becoming an invisible leap.
