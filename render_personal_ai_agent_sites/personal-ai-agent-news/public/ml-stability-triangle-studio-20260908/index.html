Three lines define the region

Write x(k+2) = trace x(k+1) - determinant x(k). Its companion matrix is [[trace, -determinant], [1, 0]], with characteristic polynomial z^2 - trace z + determinant. For real coefficients, strict stability is equivalent to the three positive margins above.

Their intersection is the open triangle with vertices (-2, 1), (2, 1), and (0, -1). The sloping edges contain a root at -1 or +1; the top segment contains roots of modulus one. A boundary point is not asymptotically stable. It need not grow: distinct unit-circle roots can stay bounded, while the repeated-root companion matrix at (2, 1) permits linear growth.

This describes a fixed linear recurrence, not a guarantee for nonlinear or time-varying learning systems. Roots are computed with ml-matrix; displayed boundary classification uses a 1e-9 numerical tolerance.
