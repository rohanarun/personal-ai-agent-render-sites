Explain groups, not just columns.

Feature importance can answer useful questions, but correlation changes the meaning of an individual attribution. A low score may mean “unneeded,” or it may mean “another feature carried the same signal.”

Use multiple views, perturbation tests, and domain knowledge. Treat attribution as evidence about model behavior, not a direct readout of causal responsibility.
