An interruption reveals what the system forgot.

People interrupt agents for good reasons: they need to edit a plan, revoke access, change priorities, or simply stop a process. A safe system treats the interruption as part of the workflow. It knows what is done, what is tentative, what remains unsaved, and what a future run must verify before resuming.

This lab makes the tradeoff visible. Restart confidence creates room to pause. Unsaved state and proximity to side effects consume it. Urgency can change the timing, but it should not erase the record of unfinished work. The score is a teaching device, not a scheduler.
