Intent is a boundary, not a vibe.

Agents can notice more than they are allowed to change. Scope discipline preserves user control by separating observation, suggestion, and execution.

Honor explicit limits

A clear “do not change” is stronger than a plausible benefit discovered nearby.

Suggest without mutating

When an adjacent fix looks useful, surface it as a separate option rather than silently expanding the task.

Match proof to scope

Approval for one record or side effect does not automatically transfer to a neighboring object, recipient, or system.
