Handoff is a contract, not a shrug.

When one agent stops and another takes over, the transfer itself is a safety boundary. Tune the four inputs below to test whether the next actor has enough context to continue without guessing.

Handoff inputs

84
Can the recipient tell what outcome is still required?
76
Are observations, decisions, and open questions recorded?
81
Does the next actor have the authority and tools to continue?
72
Can the transfer be inspected, corrected, or safely resumed?

Contract status

Handoff with a checkpoint

handoff = min(clarity, state, fit, recovery)

Why the minimum?

A handoff can fail from one missing ingredient. Averaging would let excellent task clarity conceal an undocumented state or an impossible recovery path. The weakest boundary sets the contract you can honestly make.
