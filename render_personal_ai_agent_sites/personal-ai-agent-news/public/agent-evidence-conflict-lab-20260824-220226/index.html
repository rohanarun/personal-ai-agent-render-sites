What to notice

Repeated is not independent.
Five pages copying one source should not outweigh one strong, independently gathered counterexample.
Quality changes weight.
Confidence should reflect how a source was produced, not just how often its sentence appears.
Disagreement can be information.
Silencing a conflict may make a response smoother while making the decision worse.
Try the failure case.
Push disagreement high and lower quality and independence. The answer should stop pretending the evidence is settled.
