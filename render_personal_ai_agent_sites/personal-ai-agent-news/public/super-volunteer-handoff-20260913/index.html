Before the next shift

An owner field is a proposed responsibility, not proof that someone accepted it. Ask the person to confirm. For blocked tasks, record the dependency in the next-step field; do not silently relabel them complete to make the list look clean.

Illustrative example: the welcome table is blocked because the venue has not confirmed the entrance. The handoff should say who will ask, what answer is needed, and where the reply will be recorded. Keep attendee lists and contact details in your organization's approved system, not this demo.

Technology beyond a checklist

Product demonstration, not a recording of this handoff tool.

The inspected implementation includes a verified workflow cache. Storage requires a verified-complete confirmation, runnable workflow steps and portability checks. A matching lookup can return that workflow with verification evidence and a helper-version identifier for reuse.

Missing inputs, an empty cache, or no safe match produce a cache miss. The lookup itself does not execute a fallback; the caller must handle the miss. Reuse is conditional, not a guarantee for every event task. This source inspection does not prove live availability for your account, a measured speedup, or free execution.
