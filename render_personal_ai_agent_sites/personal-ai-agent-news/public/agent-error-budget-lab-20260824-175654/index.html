Recovery is a resource, not a mood.

When an agent hits an error, the next action is shaped by more than the error itself. A frequent but harmless failure may deserve a retry. A rare but irreversible failure may deserve a hard stop. The useful question is how much capacity remains for another mistake and whether the system knows what went wrong.

This lab makes that tradeoff visible. Diagnosis and recovery capacity create room to continue. Frequency and consequence consume it. The score is a teaching device, not a reliability SLO; use it to make hidden exhaustion visible before the workflow crosses a safety boundary.
