Metrics need a mission.

Accuracy compresses different mistakes into one number. Threshold selection makes the hidden policy visible: what counts as an acceptable miss, and who absorbs the cost when the model is wrong?

Use a validation set to study the tradeoff, then choose an operating point with the people responsible for the outcome. A “best” threshold without a cost context is unfinished engineering.
