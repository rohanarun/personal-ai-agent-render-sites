Quarter-turn image desk

A quick orientation pass for photographers: rotate the whole image without dragging crop handles. Keep the original and export a separate PNG.

No image selected.

PNG, JPEG or WebP. Up to 20 MB and 24 million decoded pixels. The preview fits the desk; the PNG retains the rotated pixel dimensions.

Make orientation explicit

Illustrative scenario: a photographer receives a sideways product image for a proof sheet. Confirm which edge belongs at the top, rotate in 90-degree steps, then inspect the downloaded PNG before handing it off. A quarter turn swaps width and height; two turns preserve dimensions but reverse orientation.

The starting orientation is what the browser decoder produces, which may already account for image orientation metadata. Reset returns to that decoded starting image, not necessarily the raw sensor layout. No automatic upright detection is performed.

This is a new raster export, not a metadata-only or original-file-preserving operation. It does not preserve original EXIF or guarantee identical color-profile handling. Animated formats are outside the supported workflow. No cropping or resizing is requested, and transparency is retained where supported by the decoded image.
