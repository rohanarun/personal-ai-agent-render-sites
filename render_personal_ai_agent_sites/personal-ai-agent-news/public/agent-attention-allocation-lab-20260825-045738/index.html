Give the signal a job

A focused agent does not collect every observation. It ranks signals by what they can change and keeps uncertainty visible.

Focus on leverage

Prioritize the observation that can change the plan, risk, or next tool call rather than the loudest event.

Sharpen the question

Ask for a more specific signal when a consequential decision depends on ambiguous evidence.

Ignore with intent

Drop low-leverage noise explicitly so it does not crowd out the context needed for the real decision.

This simulation is a teaching model, not a production attention policy. It demonstrates a contract-first pattern: rank decision leverage, discount noise, and make focus choices explicit.
