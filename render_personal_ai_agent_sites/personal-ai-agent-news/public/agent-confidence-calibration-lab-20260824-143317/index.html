Confidence calibration
A small systems simulator

Confidence should track outcomes, not just sound certain.

Adjust how confident an agent sounds and how often its predictions are correct. The lab makes calibration visible: a useful confidence score should be honest about uncertainty.

Set the forecast

Confidence is a forecast of correctness. Accuracy is what happens when the same kind of forecast is tested repeatedly.

Overconfident: the forecast promises more than the outcomes support.

Read the gap

Teal is observed accuracy. Coral is stated confidence. A small gap is easier to trust than a polished overclaim.
