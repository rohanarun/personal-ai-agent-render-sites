When delegation fits
A bounded public-source comparison can be a useful trial. Define the websites, required evidence and stopping point before asking an assistant to research. Keep interpretation and the final client recommendation under your review.
If the task depends on confidential interviews, restricted portals or client credentials, stop and resolve access and sharing permissions first. This page offers no confidentiality guarantee. An agent's output is not evidence that every source was read correctly.
What Super brings
Super's product page describes browser access, cloud apps and sandboxes for practical tasks, including research. Its computer-use cache is intended to reuse successful app actions. Confirm support for your exact task before relying on it.
