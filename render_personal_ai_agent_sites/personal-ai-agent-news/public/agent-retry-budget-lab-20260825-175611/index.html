Retries need a budget.

Trying again can recover a transient failure or amplify a bad plan. Adjust failure signal, strategy change, recovery cost, and observability to see when an agent should retry, rethink, or escalate.

Retry posture

Retry with a changed plan

retry = min(signal, change, cost, trace)

Why the minimum?

A retry is useful only when its failure signal is informative, its strategy changes, and its cost is contained. Taking the minimum prevents repeated attempts from masquerading as progress.
