Good planning is selective, not exhaustive.

Agents need enough search to find a useful route and enough pruning to keep their reasoning legible. The right budget depends on stakes, cost, and how much a human can review.

A working rule
Spend search where another branch can change the decision.

Bound the tree, preserve the reason for pruning, and stop when new options no longer improve the choice.
