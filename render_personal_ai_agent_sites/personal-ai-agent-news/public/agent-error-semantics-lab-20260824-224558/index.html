What to notice

Symptoms invite bad retries.
“Request failed” does not tell a policy whether to repair input, wait, or stop.
Recovery signals are affordances.
An error should reveal whether a safe next action exists, not just report that the last one ended.
Impact makes errors actionable.
Users need to know what changed, what did not, and whether a partial side effect occurred.
