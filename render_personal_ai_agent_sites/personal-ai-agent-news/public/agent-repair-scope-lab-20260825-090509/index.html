Repair the right layer.

A failed outcome does not tell an agent how wide the repair should be. The right scope fixes the violated condition without disturbing the parts that still work.

Good repair is surgical when the failure is local, and systemic when the local symptom hides a broken assumption.

Preserve working state

Repair the narrowest layer that can restore the violated condition. Unnecessary resets erase useful evidence.

Widen on evidence

Broaden the repair when repeated local fixes point to a shared assumption or a system-level mismatch.

Verify the outcome

Do not confuse a completed repair operation with a recovered state. Re-run the postcondition that mattered.
