Stereo Channel Swap

How to exchange left and right without rebuilding a short audio clip in a multitrack session.

Stereo only. Up to 8 MiB and 30 seconds after decoding. Browser-supported formats; output is 48 kHz, 32-bit float WAV. Start playback at a comfortable low volume.

Two channels, exchanged

The exported left channel contains the decoded right-channel samples; the exported right contains the decoded left. The tool does not sum channels, change polarity, denoise, normalize, or detect whether your source was wired incorrectly. The player initially uses 30% volume, but export does not apply that preview volume.

The original sample is two seconds: a 440 Hz tone in the left channel during the first second, then an 880 Hz tone in the right channel during the second. It includes short fades. The waveform follows the selected audition; export always writes the swapped version.

Browser decoding resamples to the 48 kHz offline audio context used here. Therefore this is not a byte-preserving file edit, and the decoded sample rate may differ from the source. Metadata is not carried forward. A float WAV does not add detail to a compressed recording. Listen to the result in its intended player; some software has limited float-WAV support.
