A survivor is a hypothesis

At zero balance, adopt the next label. A matching vote increases balance; a different vote decreases it. Opposing votes cancel. If a label occurs more than half the time, it survives as the final candidate.

Why the second pass matters

Without a majority guarantee, count the candidate's actual occurrences and test whether they exceed n/2. The balance is not its frequency. Exactly half is a tie, not a strict majority.

Consensus is not correctness

These are synthetic agent-answer labels, not independent measurements of truth. Even a verified majority may repeat a shared mistake. This experiment verifies only a frequency claim, not the correctness or independence of the answers.

What is actually stored?

The streaming algorithm needs a candidate and counter. This teaching interface additionally retains the input and trace for replay, export, and the second pass. A real non-replayable stream needs another way to retain or recover votes before performing verification.
