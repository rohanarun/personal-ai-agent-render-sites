Evidence is a boundary, not a mood.

An agent should not turn a plausible observation into a confident action by skipping the middle steps. Tune the evidence quality, uncertainty, action risk, and verification below to see when the right move is to act, ask, or stop.

Recommended mode

Act with a visible check

decision confidence = min(evidence, certainty, risk, verify)

Why the minimum?

One weak boundary can dominate the decision. An average score would let a polished source or a low-risk action hide missing uncertainty handling. Reliable agents keep the weakest link visible and change mode before changing the world.
