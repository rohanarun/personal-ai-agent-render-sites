What the simulation teaches

An assumption ledger is a small memory of what the agent believes, why it believes it, and what observation would change its mind.

A useful design move

Give each assumption a confidence, an evidence link, and a refresh condition. When the environment changes, refresh the fragile beliefs instead of replaying the entire plan.
