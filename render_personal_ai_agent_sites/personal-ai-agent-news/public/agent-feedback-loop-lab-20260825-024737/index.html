What the simulation teaches

Feedback is not just logging. It is the signal that tells an agent whether its action moved the system toward the goal or made the next decision less reliable.

A useful design move

Measure the outcome closest to the action, keep the feedback window short, and make corrections proportional. A delayed, noisy signal can turn a helpful loop into oscillation.
