Three ways to stay on the frontier.

Make the policy choice concrete enough to evaluate, inspect, and change.

Make review meaningful

A human gate should add context or authority, not become a ceremonial click after the decision is already made.

Spend evidence where impact peaks

Reserve deeper checks for actions whose downside is large, irreversible, or hard to observe after the fact.

Measure the tradeoff

Evaluate speed, reliability, oversight load, and user harm together instead of celebrating one metric alone.
