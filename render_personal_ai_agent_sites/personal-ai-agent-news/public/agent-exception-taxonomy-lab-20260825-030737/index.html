What the simulation teaches

Exception types are decision inputs. A transient timeout, an invalid argument, and a permission denial may all look like failure, but they demand different next actions.

A useful design move

Attach a type, severity, retry hint, and safe owner to every failure. A typed exception makes the next policy explicit instead of asking the agent to infer it from prose.
