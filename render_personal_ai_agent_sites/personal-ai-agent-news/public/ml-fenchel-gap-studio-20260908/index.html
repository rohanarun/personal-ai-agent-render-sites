From conjugacy to divergence

G(z,p) = log Σᵢ exp(zᵢ) + Σᵢ pᵢ log pᵢ − pᵀz
G(z,p) = Σᵢ pᵢ log(pᵢ/qᵢ) ≥ 0

Log-sum-exp is the convex conjugate of negative entropy on the probability simplex. The conjugate definition says that no candidate p can exceed its optimal score-minus-negative-entropy value. The maximizing distribution is softmax.

Substitute log qᵢ = zᵢ − log Σ exp(z) to obtain the KL identity. All qᵢ are positive for these finite scores. The convention 0 log 0 = 0 makes one-hot candidates valid. Equality holds exactly when p = q in exact arithmetic.

This also distinguishes cross-entropy from the gap: cross-entropy is −Σpᵢ log qᵢ, whereas the gap subtracts the entropy of p. They coincide for a one-hot p, but not for a general soft distribution.
