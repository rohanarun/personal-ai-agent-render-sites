Make cancellation travel

A delegated agent should know who granted its authority, what scope it inherited, and how a parent withdrawal reaches it.

  • Attach a parent identity and expiry to child work.
  • Propagate cancellation through every handoff.
  • Stop side effects before cleaning up the explanation.

Try the trap

Raise inherited scope while keeping cancellation low. The child may remain active after the parent’s intent has changed.
