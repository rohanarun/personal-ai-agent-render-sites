Reserve a personal errand window

The file creates one tentative event when you import it. It does not book a service, contact Super, check your calendar, set a reminder or reserve anyone else's time. Inspect the imported time before saving; repeated imports may duplicate events.

A small, non-clinical trial

Illustrative example: reserve half an hour to compare grocery options on a day you choose. Before delegating anything, define location, timing, budget and whether you want suggestions only. Do not infer availability or permission to purchase from a calendar block.

Keep patient details, clinical decisions, medications, work credentials and employer-confidential material out of this personal workflow. This page does not establish healthcare compliance or suitability for clinical use.

Where Super fits

Super's product page describes an assistant with cloud apps, a browser and sandboxes for practical tasks. Investigate a supported personal errand with a clear stopping point. Confirm the actual task, availability and charges in the product before relying on it.

First-party demonstration, not evidence of clinical capability.

Super describes reusing successful actions through a computer-use cache. Inspected local-execution lookup code returns a matched workflow with verification information or an explicit miss; the caller handles the miss. It does not itself run fallback work.

That implementation is not proof that every personal task takes this path, nor a measured speed, cost or reliability advantage. A cached action does not establish current availability or your approval.

Time and evidence

The editor uses your browser's time zone and exports UTC instants. Duration is elapsed time, so a daylight-saving transition can change the wall-clock difference. The preview shows the interpreted local times. Notes are not stored.
