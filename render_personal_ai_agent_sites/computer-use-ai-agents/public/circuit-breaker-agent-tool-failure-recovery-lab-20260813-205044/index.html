Failure becomes a state, not a surprise.

The breaker does not repair the tool. It gives the dependency room to recover and gives the agent a fast, explicit signal to choose a fallback.

Closed

Measure real outcomes.

Calls flow normally. Consecutive failures accumulate until the configured threshold turns uncertainty into a known outage.

Open

Reject without touching the tool.

During cooldown, the agent fails fast. It can defer work, use cached context, or choose another tool without adding load.

Half-open

Spend one probe on recovery.

After cooldown, a successful probe closes the circuit. A failed probe immediately reopens it and starts a new recovery window.
