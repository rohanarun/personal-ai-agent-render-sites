SPRT is designed for two simple hypotheses. The lab exposes the exact Bernoulli likelihood increments and Wald’s approximate stopping thresholds.

Accumulate evidence

Success adds evidence according to p₁/p₀; failure adds evidence according to the complementary probabilities.

LLRₙ = Σ log[P(xᵢ|H₁)/P(xᵢ|H₀)]

Roll out

Cross the upper boundary when evidence favors the target strongly enough.

A = log[(1−β)/α]

Reject

Cross the lower boundary when the baseline explanation dominates.

B = log[β/(1−α)]

Deployment cautions

  • Choose p₀ and p₁ before observing rollout data.
  • Account for nonstationarity and dependent outcomes.
  • Record every stop and restart to avoid optional-stopping abuse.
  • Use operational guardrails alongside statistical evidence.
