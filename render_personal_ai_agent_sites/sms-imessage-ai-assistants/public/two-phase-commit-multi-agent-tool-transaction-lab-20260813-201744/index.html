One decision, two phases.

The protocol separates readiness from outcome. That gives atomicity, but a participant can block while waiting for a crashed coordinator’s durable decision.

1

Prepare and vote.

The coordinator asks each participant whether it can commit. A YES vote means the participant durably records intent and holds the resources needed to finish.

2

Record one global outcome.

All YES votes produce COMMIT. Any NO vote or timeout produces ABORT. The coordinator writes that decision durably before announcing it.

3

Recover, then finish.

After a crash, the coordinator replays its log and resends the same decision. If it failed before recording one, prepared participants remain uncertain and blocked.
