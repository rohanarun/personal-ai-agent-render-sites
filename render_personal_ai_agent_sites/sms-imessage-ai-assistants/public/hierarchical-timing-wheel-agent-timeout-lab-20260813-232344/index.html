Deadline scheduling for agent systems

Bucket the deadlines. Advance one timeout slot at a time.

A hierarchical timing wheel groups nearby agent timeouts into cheap buckets and holds distant deadlines in a coarser level. Advance the clock to fire, cancel, and cascade thousands of timers without scanning every task.

Precision belongs near now. Range belongs farther out.

Each wheel level trades resolution for reach, so deadline work stays proportional to active buckets rather than the entire timer population.

Choose a level from remaining delay.

Near deadlines land in the fine wheel. Distant deadlines occupy coarser slots that cover larger time ranges.

Advance

Inspect only the current bucket.

Each clock tick moves one cursor and examines the linked timers stored in that slot, avoiding a full scan.

Cascade

Promote deadlines as they approach.

When the fine wheel wraps, timers from the next level are reinserted with finer resolution until they are ready to fire.
