Fair scheduling for variable-cost agent work

Carry unused credit forward. Serve every tool queue fairly.

Deficit round robin gives each agent queue a quantum, spends credit only on jobs it can afford, and carries the remainder into the next round. Small and large tool calls share capacity without starving.

Fairness comes from remembering what each queue could not spend.

Deficit counters turn fixed round-robin visits into cost-aware service for indivisible jobs.

Credit

Add one quantum to every active queue.

A larger configured quantum gives a queue more service over time, while empty queues leave the active list.

Spend

Serve head jobs that fit the counter.

Each completed tool call subtracts its cost. A job that is too large waits without blocking visits to other queues.

Carry

Keep the leftover deficit for the next round.

Accumulated credit eventually pays for large jobs, preserving throughput fairness without splitting the work item.
