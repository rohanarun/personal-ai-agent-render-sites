The model learns a direction, not a density value.

The score is the gradient of log probability with respect to the sample. Noise makes the target field well behaved off the data manifold, and the Gaussian corruption process provides a tractable denoising direction for supervision.

Denoising supplies the target vector.

Corrupt a clean sample with Gaussian noise. The conditional score of that corruption points directly from the noisy observation back toward its source.

target(x_t, x_0, sigma) = -(x_t - x_0) / sigma^2

A ladder of scales connects static to structure.

Large noise bridges isolated modes; small noise sharpens local detail.

Score matching

Fit a noise-conditioned network to the denoising target. Weighting across sigma levels controls which scales dominate training.

E[lambda(sigma) ||s_theta(x_t,sigma)-target||^2]

Langevin dynamics

Each sampling step combines drift along the score with fresh Gaussian randomness.

x_(k+1) = x_k + epsilon s_theta(x_k) + sqrt(2 epsilon) z

Reverse diffusion

In continuous time, the learned score modifies the reverse-time drift of a diffusion process.

dx = [f(x,t)-g(t)^2 score(x,t)]dt + g(t)dW_bar

The wrong field can still look smooth.

Missing modes remain invisible.

If training data or model capacity omit a region, the learned score can redirect samples toward represented modes. Attractive trajectories are not proof of distributional coverage.

Step size couples bias and mixing.

Large Langevin steps move quickly but discretize the target dynamics poorly. Tiny steps reduce discretization error while increasing compute and risking slow movement between modes.

Low-noise error scars the sample.

Near the data manifold, small vector errors determine fine detail. A schedule that descends faster than the score can support may freeze artifacts into the final sample.

Coverage
Count modes and missing regions.

Use class-conditional, neighborhood, and precision-recall diagnostics rather than one aggregate fidelity score.

Calibration
Inspect vector error by sigma.

Average loss can hide severe low-noise or tail errors. Track magnitude and angular quality over space and noise level.

Sampling
Separate model and solver error.

Compare samplers and step budgets with a fixed score network. Numerical quality and learned-field quality are distinct.

Safety
Audit memorization and data rights.

High-fidelity generation can reproduce training examples or sensitive attributes. Evaluate nearest neighbors, provenance, and intended use.
