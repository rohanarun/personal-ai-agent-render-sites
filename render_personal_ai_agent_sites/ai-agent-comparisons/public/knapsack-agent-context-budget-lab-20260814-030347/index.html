What the simple knapsack leaves out.

Dependencies

A tool result may be useless without its query, and a policy may unlock several memories. Production packing may need grouped or prerequisite-aware constraints.

Redundancy

Two individually useful memories may repeat the same fact. Submodular value models can discount overlap rather than adding utilities independently.

Uncertain utility

Utility is predicted before generation. Logging which blocks actually influence outcomes is necessary to recalibrate future packing decisions.
