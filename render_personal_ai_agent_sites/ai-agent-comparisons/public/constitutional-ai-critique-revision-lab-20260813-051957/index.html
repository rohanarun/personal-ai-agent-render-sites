A rule is not a result.

A constitution names desired behavior. A critique identifies which principle a draft violates and why. A revision attempts to reduce that violation while preserving useful content. An audit checks the new tradeoff rather than assuming the rewrite worked.

Read the audit trail, not just the verdict.

The same “safe” outcome can come from a precise revision or a blanket refusal. The audit trail distinguishes targeted risk reduction from deleting everything useful.
