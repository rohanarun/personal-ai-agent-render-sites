Memorization is not one thing.

A model can learn a reusable pattern from many examples. It can also assign exceptional likelihood to one rare training sequence. An attacker who knows part of that sequence can rank completions. Exposure measures how sharply the secret stands out.

Inspect the record-level risk.

Common phrases are easy to reproduce without proving memorization. Rare, high-entropy strings with exceptional rank provide stronger evidence of record-level exposure.
