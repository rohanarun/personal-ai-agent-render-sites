Diversity helps only when members make meaningfully different mistakes.

Aggregate distributions, not labels.

Every member emits a predictive distribution. Averaging those distributions preserves confidence structure and lets between-member variance expose model uncertainty.

p(y|x) ≈ 1/M Σ p(y|x, θm)

Correlation limits the gain.

Twelve near-identical members can repeat one blind spot. Distinct initializations, data orders, augmentations, or snapshots must produce functionally different errors.

Epistemic uncertainty

Member disagreement tends to rise where training evidence is weak or deployment inputs have shifted.

Aleatoric uncertainty

All members may agree that an observation is noisy. Ensemble size cannot remove irreducible ambiguity.

Abstention policy

Calibrate disagreement thresholds against coverage, accepted-case risk, latency, and important cohorts.

Five copies are not always five perspectives.

Correlated ensemble

Members share the same error direction. Confidence looks stable, but disagreement understates risk during a common failure.

Diverse ensemble

Members fit the task while exploring different solutions. Their spread becomes informative when the query leaves familiar support.
