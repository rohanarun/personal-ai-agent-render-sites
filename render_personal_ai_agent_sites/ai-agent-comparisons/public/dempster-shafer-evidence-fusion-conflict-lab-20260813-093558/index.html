Evidence is not always probability

Commit only what each source can support.

A sensor may support “safe,” another may support “unsafe,” and either may reserve mass for “I cannot distinguish.” Belief is committed support. Plausibility adds everything not yet ruled out. Their gap is explicit ignorance.

Read the interval

Three quantities tell different truths.

Belief is guaranteed support.

For “safe,” belief counts mass committed exactly to safe. It refuses to borrow from unresolved evidence.

Plausibility is what remains possible.

Plausibility adds unresolved mass because it does not contradict safe. The interval between belief and plausibility is not random noise; it is missing commitment.

Conflict belongs in the report.

When one source says safe and another says unsafe, Dempster normalization redistributes non-conflicting mass. Under extreme conflict that can look overconfident, so alternative rules retain the conflict as ignorance.

Where fusion fails

Dependent sources

Combining duplicated evidence as if independent double-counts support. Trace provenance before fusion.

Total contradiction

As conflict approaches one, normalized fusion becomes numerically and conceptually fragile.

Bad frames

If the true hypothesis is missing from the frame of discernment, ignorance cannot rescue the model.
