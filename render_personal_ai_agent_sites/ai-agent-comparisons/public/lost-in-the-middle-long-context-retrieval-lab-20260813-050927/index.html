Presence is not access.

A model can accept the tokens, yet fail to route enough attention or computation to use the relevant passage.

Distractor similarity

Semantically similar chunks compete with the evidence and can capture retrieval or attention despite being wrong.

Retrieval tradeoff

RAG shortens the effective context but introduces embedding, chunking, recall, and ranking failure modes.

Evaluation design

Vary position, depth, relevance, answer type, and multi-hop structure. A single needle test is not a complete long-context audit.

Choose the failure surface you can measure.

Full context

No retrieval miss, but quadratic attention cost and positional bias grow with length.

Retrieve

Move likely evidence near the query. Fast and focused, but embedding recall decides what the model can see.

Rerank

Spend more inference on candidate order. Better precision, added latency, and another scorer to calibrate.
