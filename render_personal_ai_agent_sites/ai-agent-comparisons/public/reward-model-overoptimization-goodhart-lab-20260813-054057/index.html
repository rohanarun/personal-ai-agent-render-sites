Goodhart arrives through selection.

A reward model approximates human preference on familiar outputs. Optimization searches for higher scores. Strong search moves into unusual outputs where model error is larger. The proxy can keep climbing after real quality turns downward.

Proxy reward versus true quality

The acid point is the selected output. The gap between the two curves is exploitable reward-model error.
