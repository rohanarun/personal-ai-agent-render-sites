Surface diversity is not epistemic diversity.

One belief can be expressed with many synonyms and sentence structures. Token-level entropy can rise while semantic confidence remains stable.

Equivalence errors

A clustering model can merge subtly contradictory claims or split compatible answers, directly biasing the uncertainty estimate.

Sampling coverage

Too few samples may miss plausible alternative meanings. More samples help only when decoding explores the relevant answer space.

Abstention policy

Calibrate semantic entropy against downstream risk, question type, coverage, and the cost of refusing a correct answer.
