Guidance extrapolates a score difference.

One diffusion model is trained with and without the condition by randomly dropping conditioning inputs. At sampling time, two network evaluations estimate a baseline score and a conditional score. Their difference becomes a direction that can be amplified.

The prompt becomes a vector in score space.

Scale one leaves the conditional estimate unchanged. Larger scales move beyond it, often improving prompt adherence while distorting the original conditional distribution.

score_cfg = score_uncond + w (score_cond - score_uncond)

One baseline, several steering directions.

Target and negative conditions alter the direction, not merely a final ranking.

Condition dropout

Training on null and real conditions lets one network produce both estimates required for guidance.

c_tilde = c with probability 1-p, else null

Negative guidance

A negative prompt can replace or augment the unconditional baseline, pushing away from an undesired condition.

score = score_neg + w(score_pos-score_neg)

Thresholding

High guidance can create extreme predictions. Static or dynamic thresholding clips or rescales outputs to control saturation.

x_hat = clip(x, -s, s) / s

More prompt is not always more truth.

High scale narrows the distribution.

Guidance concentrates mass around prompt-compatible regions. Alignment may rise while variation, rare submodes, and compositional alternatives disappear.

Extrapolation leaves the training field.

The model is trained to estimate conditional and unconditional scores, not arbitrarily amplified combinations. Very high scales can push trajectories into poorly modeled regions.

Negative prompts can entangle concepts.

Moving away from one text condition does not isolate a single visual property. Correlated features in the learned representation may be suppressed or exaggerated together.

Evaluate the distribution, not one favorite sample.

Alignment
Measure prompt satisfaction by facet.

Separate object, relation, style, count, and attribute adherence. One similarity score can hide composition failures.

Diversity
Sample enough seeds.

Track intra-prompt variation, mode coverage, and duplication across scale. A compelling cherry-picked image proves little.

Artifacts
Inspect saturation and clipping.

Guidance and thresholding interact with sampler, parameterization, and decoder. Audit extreme colors and fine-detail collapse.

Safety
Test positive and negative wording.

Guidance can amplify demographic associations, unsafe concepts, or memorized patterns. Evaluate across prompt families and languages.
