Intervene, then measure causally.

Add or subtract the vector during the forward pass. A probe can correlate with a concept without controlling it; intervention tests whether the direction changes behavior.

Layer matters

Early layers may not encode the concept cleanly. Late layers can leave little room for the intervention to shape generation.

Scale is nonlinear

More steering is not always more useful. Large coefficients can collapse fluency or activate entangled attributes.

Pairs define the vector

Biased or narrow contrasts can package topic, register, and formatting into what appears to be one semantic direction.

Audit three failure surfaces before deployment.

Entanglement

The direction may carry correlated style or topic features. Test unrelated prompts and counterfactual contrast sets.

Oversteering

A coefficient that works on one prompt can saturate another. Sweep positive and negative strengths across task families.

Layer instability

Behavioral effects can peak sharply at one layer or move across model families. Scan rather than assume transfer.
